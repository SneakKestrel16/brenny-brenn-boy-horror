## 2. Conflicts

- **C1. M1 against M2 (softening twice).** Take M2's safety net and keep the payment tight (my M1 amendment). If dawn 3 can't hurt, the first act has no stakes.
- **C2. P3 against T3 (knowledge separation).** Targeted lures need true teammate distances. Resolution: separate hunting from presentation (T3 amended). The rule players can sense, "it hunts by what it hears", stays honest.
- **C3. P1 Off tier against mimicry.** A generic voice standing in for a known friend gives the fake away. Resolution: mimic Off players with sounds only (P1 amended).
- **C4. P1 tiers against P2 chatter.** Chatter is off-guard speech. Resolution: it falls under the Lobby tier only with a visible "recording" notice in the barn and per-clip review/delete. It is never kept in the host save.
- **C5. M8 guarding against T6 (lit building).** The pumpkin sits by the farmhouse, so guarding it through a window costs nothing. Resolution: the guard must be outdoors.
- **C6. P8 ghost range against the flicker's usefulness.** Too little vision and the flicker has nothing to warn about; too much and ghosts become Discord radar. Resolution: 20 m smeared silhouette plus animal and crow reactions.
- **C7. M6 dawn Taint against "Taint until dawn".** A Taint caught at dawn would last the whole day. Resolution: the unpicked flower is a Taint *object*. You only catch it by touching it, and you can wash it off.

## 3. New issues

**T12 (minor): Discord doesn't only weaken mimicry, it also removes a cost.** (Keeping Players on In-Game Voice) The creature hears in-game talking, so out-of-band chat means silent coordination with no cost and no mimicry at all. The doc's "accepted trade-off" understates this. *Change:* say it plainly. Don't let the Director cheat to compensate. Make the honest channels that only exist in-game (static ghosts, radios, positional voice) the reason to stay in-game, and add a lobby note: "the creature can't hear Discord, and you can't hear where your friends are."

**T13 (minor): the scare budget across P3 and the Director.** Targeted lures, hallucinations and the wrong count are all per-player and invisible to everyone else. Without a shared budget one player gets three private events in a row while the others get nothing. *Change:* every private event counts against the T5 per-player day cap and the Director's 2-minute spacing.
