### B2 objection

The draft says "a set distance" and stops there. The tuning decides whether every hidden trap is fair or a death sentence. A player who steps into a trap alone, unaware, must be able to survive it. Otherwise a lure into a hidden trap is a death the player had no part in, which is exactly what T2 set out to stop. Replacement wording:

> **B2. The day trap kill is a race.** When a trap springs by day, the creature's sound signature starts approaching from a set distance. **The race is tuned so that a solo, untainted player who starts prying at once survives, with a few seconds to spare.** Hesitating, being Tainted (shorter sprint means a slower pry), or the trap being deep in the corn can lose it. Pry free in time and you live, Shaken. Fail and you die. A teammate shortens the pry. The Director's distance bending (B3) applies to the start distance, within that tuning.

Without this wording, a designer can make B2 lethal by default, and the day then stops feeling like a break (B6).
