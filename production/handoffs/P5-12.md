- Loop mode imports as none. Set `LOOP_LINEAR` on idle, walk, run, crouch in code. The others are one-shots that start and end at rest.
- Part nodes Torso, Head, ArmL, ArmR, LegL, LegR still exist, now skinned mesh nodes under `Armature` with their origin at the model origin (QA P5-12: in P4-16 they sat at the joint, e.g. ArmL (-0.23, 1.5, 0)). The joints are now the bones: arm_l at (-0.23, 1.5, 0), thigh_l at (-0.1, 0.98, 0), head at 1.5 (was the Head node at 1.55). Pose the bones, not the part nodes: a skinned mesh ignores its own node transform.
- Materials: `mat_farmer_overalls` is the player tint surface (vertex colours are light grey, set `albedo_color` per player; untinted it renders cream). `mat_farmer_sleeves` (sleeves, hands) is the Taint slot. Shirt chest, head, hair, boots and brass stay `mat_flat_lit`. Colour proposal in Q-267.
- Front is -Z. Rest AABB 0.608 x 1.8 x 0.3825 (arms make it wider than doc 0.5; doc 07 updated).
