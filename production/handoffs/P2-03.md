## Done

- **Recording screen** (`game/voice/recording_screen.gd`, new). Opens on `Game.recording_requested`
  and by itself in the lobby for an unchosen player (or Lobby lines with no lines). Intro naming who
  won't be heard, the seven fixed lines from `data/voice_lines.json`, one line per teammate's name,
  20 to 40 s of barn chatter, review (play, delete). Two 3 s takes per line plus an optional third,
  trimmed by the VAD, scored with the line's `take_scoring` weights (loudness plus pitch spread by
  autocorrelation); the best take is kept, the others never leave memory. Staging cues run 0.6 s
  before the prompt (`lantern_out` blows the barn lantern out through `LightRig.stage_blown`,
  `door_bang` plays a bang). A steady "REC recording" tally sits in the title row, and every peer
  shows a steady emissive bulb over the recorder (`request/apply_recording_light`). Off players'
  voices go to the muted `VoiceMuted` bus while capturing (D-011).

  `Net.user_dir()/voice/`; Off deletes them all, clears `lines_recorded` and shares an empty
  manifest so every peer frees them (`clip_freed`); leftover clips with the setting already Off are
  deleted at start. Manifest plus chunks on channel 3 through the host, host caps and hash checks,
  late joiners get everything at join, ready digest per client, 30 s timeout that pauses while anyone
  records (inference, doc 06 s12). `Game.match_ready()` calls `Voice.clips.ready_to_start()`.
