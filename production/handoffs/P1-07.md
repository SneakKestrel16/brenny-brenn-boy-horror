## Done
- `game/core/generator.gd` (node `Generator`, added by `main.gd` after Farm and Look): host-owned `fuel_s` (tank `generator_tank_s` 210 s, doc 02 s14), `damaged` flag. Burns only at dusk and night. Registers two Interactables in `Farm.targets`: `fuel_drum` (`fill_fuel`, 3 s, free, gives the player a can in `Game.players[peer].fuel_can`) and `generator` (`refuel` 4 s, adds `fuel_can_pct` 50% of the tank, uses the can; `repair_generator` 6 s when damaged). All three go through HoldController / HoldRegistry (D-026 `request_hold`).
- Dead (fuel 0 or damaged): `NoiseBus.emit_kind(&"generator_dead")` once at the generator (80 m, source 0) and every building light goes off. Repair emits `tool_repair` (25 m). `damage()` is the hook for the creature's generator_kill (doc 03 s9); nothing calls it yet except `--autogen`.
- `game/core/lights.gd`: the only caller of `LightRig.set_on` / `set_dim`; `set_on(powered)`, `set_dim(fuel_s / tank_s)`. LightRig does the below-25% smooth dim to 35% and its own slew. No flicker anywhere: `grep_rules.py` flicker, energy_override, light_energy all ok.
- Replication: new RPC `Net.apply_generator(fuel_s, damaged)` (reliable): on change and once a second while burning; a client's `request_farm_state` also gets it, so a late joiner sees the right lights. Clients drive their own rigs from it.
- One run per night: the tank refills at each new day (`Clock.day_changed`). Inference, doc 05 s12 only says "one generator run"; DD Phase 1 feel settles it.
- Go-still: `game/player/still_ring.gd` (pure, 1 s ring of host-received positions, still = furthest sample within 0.2 m of the newest, ring must span about 1 s). `Players._ingest` pushes every frame, sets `Game.players[peer].is_still`, logs `still_changed`. Client sends no flag. Test: `tests/gameplay/test_still_ring.gd` (0.19 m still, 0.21 m not, short history not still, walk-then-stop).
- Crouch noise: unchanged from P1-04, `step_crouch` radius 0 in creature.json (no signal, counter only). Verified there is no extra work needed; footsteps come from the move stream so a client cannot suppress them.
- QA flag `-- --autogen` (both peers): host forces night with 12 s of fuel; client fetches a can, refuels, host then damages, client repairs. `--log-farm` also logs `generator_seen` on every machine.
