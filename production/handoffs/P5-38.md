## Method

## Top 5 costs (numbers, with caveat)

1. Whole frame is CPU-bound at about 2.0 to 2.4 ms with 4 instances sharing the machine; GPU is 0.5 to 0.8 ms (a quarter of it).
2. Road view: 403 to 418 draws and 172k tris, the largest scene cost; CPU render 0.47 ms (2x the other spots). Still 27% of the draw budget.
3. Corn lane: 92k tris with only 48 to 65 draws (corn MultiMesh, fine).
4. Physics monitor 0.5 to 1.4 ms (a per-second maximum, not a per-frame cost; inference, see 07_gotchas.md) (host at night 1.43 ms, creature and bots).
5. Occasional frame spikes of 12 to 16 ms (a few per minute, no frame over 33 ms). Source not found; vsync-on runs showed none.

Per-script ranking was attempted (`--probe-census`: switch one script group off for 2 s) but the monitors (per-second maxima, so not per-frame costs) (about 0.3 ms, plus 100 ms stalls) is bigger than any one script's cost, so no script can be named a hotspot. Read of `light_rig`, `world_props`, `settings_apply` `_process`: cheap (a few ops, `world_props` throttles to 2 Hz). Needs the editor profiler to go further.
