# P5-69 Creature as little visible as possible by day (AI Programmer)

CEO D-188 (D-1, D-18): "the creature should be the least visible possible", "dash across, limiting view
of it in daytime". Doc 01 "What players see": by day parts and motion only. After P5-65, QA still saw the
whole boar walk across open lanes between corn blocks for up to 8.5 s (seeds 2 and 4).

## What was done

All in `game/creature/creature.gd` `_move()`, day lurk only (`_night_t < 0.0`, so day, dusk and dawn, the
same span as `lurk_open_cost_mult_day`):

1. **Dash.** Out of the `World/CornBlockers` corn, a day `lurk` moves at `lurk_dash_speed_day` (new key,
   placeholder 5.5 m/s = chase speed) until it is back in corn. Skipped on a map with no corn (Phase 1
   farm), where it would otherwise dash everywhere.
2. **Hide.** At the corn's edge (in corn now, out of corn 1 m along its heading), it checks the next leg
   (`_way_to` waypoint) every 3 m (`WATCH_STEP_M`). If any living player's eye (1.65 m) has a clear ray
   (layers 1 and 5) to the creature's chest (1.2 m, `CHEST_M`) at an open point, it stands still in the
   corn. After `lurk_hide_wait_s` (new key, placeholder 10 s) still watched, it drops the route and picks
   another (the `wander_recent_s` rule keeps it off the same point). The wait returns before the stuck
   check, so it logs no `creature_stuck`.
   `_watched()` uses **true positions**. It counts as presentation: it only hides, never steers toward a
   player, so hunting still runs on what the creature senses (doc 03 s3). There is no debug-view line for
   it; the Director can ask for one if QA wants the difference shown.

Route costs (`_open_mult`, `corn_route`, `hop_costs`) are untouched: the hide check at the edge was smaller
than a per-player cost on hops, and it reacts to players who move.
