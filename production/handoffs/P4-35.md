## Done
- The menu lobby (`game/ui/lobby.tscn`, `lobby.gd`) now draws a 3D line-up behind its panels (D-140). Layout only was taken from the CEO's reference; the palette is dark planks, blue moonlight and warm lanterns.
- Set (`_stage`, placeholder boxes): plank floor and back wall, two posts and a beam, a moonlit window, hay. Light: three `LightRig` lanterns on the beam (the centre one in group `barn_lantern`, so the recording screen's `lantern_out` step blows it out, Q-176), plus a blue moon and a warm spot on the centre farmer. The moon and the spot are authored in `lobby.tscn` because only `game/render/` may set `light_energy` in code (doc 07 s4.4 rule 3; grep_rules reports the scene values as warnings). Ambient is the doc 07 s5 night floor, 0.25. Nothing in the lobby changes a light on its own.
- `Players` is now `lobby.gd` class `LineUp`: one placeholder farmer per player (capsule body, head, arms) with a role hat (`LineUp.HATS`, one colour/brim/crown per role, Q-241) and three `Label3D` tags: name, role (upper case, blank for no role), and HOST (peer 1), READY (bots and `Game.lobby_ready`) or NOT READY. The local farmer stands in the centre; the others alternate right and left, 1.15 m apart, each 0.35 m further back. `sync()` runs on every lobby refresh and on `roles_changed`; it frees the farmer of anyone no longer in `Game.players`.
- Voice hangs each emitter on that player's farmer (`LineUp.player(peer)`, the same call as before), so lobby voice is now placed; the AudioListener3D rides the camera.
- UI: "THE LOBBY" title; left panel role cards; right panel "Farmhands" roster (name, host/you, voice setting, recording), join code, Discord line, group settings, Settings and Leave; a large bottom-right button: host "START THE SEASON" (or "Waiting for everyone to be ready"), client "READY" / "READY, waiting for the host" on two lines.

- OPEN_ISSUES "Found at the P4-23 review" item 4 fixed: a leaver's farmer (and the VoiceEmitter under it) is freed by `LineUp.sync()`.
- Doc 01 "Staging" and "Picking a role" say "menu lobby" (D-140). Doc 05 s16 describes the line-up, the placed lobby voice and the lock key.
