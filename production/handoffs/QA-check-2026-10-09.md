## Findings

1. **5 and 6 players are too easy (Game Designer).** P4-02 tuned 2 to 4 players. At 5p and 6p the
   final payment clears in about 83% of seasons against the 55 to 70% target, and the spread to 2 to 4
   players (about 21 points) breaks the "at most 10 points" target. The Prize Pumpkin barely matters at
   6p (Medium vs Large: 2.6 points). Doc 01 says "5 and 6 supported and scaled". Suspects:
   `payment_pct_by_players` 5 = 120, 6 = 140 and `field_plots_start_by_players` 20 / 24
   (`data/player_scaling.json`). The sim runs one player count per call, so the cross-count spread
   check never compares 5p/6p with 2p to 4p. Settle by retuning in `tools/sim/` with `--players 5` and
   `--players 6`.
2. **Headless runs on Linux need the TwoVoIP Linux library.** The pinned archive has it; the repo keeps
   Windows only (D-009). Not a bug; noted for anyone running CI or a cloud check.
3. **Not covered by any automated run:** deaths, respawn and ghosts in a full season (autowalk players
   never died); lures and the trap race (none fired with autowalk); real voice; rendering and frame
   time. These need the dev console script from the P3-13 runs or human play.

## What a playtest has to test

Everything below is unproven until people play it. Sources: doc 01 "Build Plan", doc 09 s3, D-057,
D-068, TASKS P4-01 and P4-18, `tools/qa/playtest/checklist_p2.md` and `checklist_p3.md`.

### Session rules (doc 09 s2)

- At least 2 sessions per phase; at least one tester who has not read doc 01 and was told nothing.
- 2 to 4 players per session; one session on different home networks; real mics and headphones.
- Every peer's log collected; observer silent, counting screams, laughs and bored silences.
- Same five debrief questions every session (doc 09 s2).

### Phase 4 "done when" (doc 01, doc 09 s3 DD Phase 4)

- [ ] **Teams sometimes win and sometimes lose:** at least 6 full seasons, 2 each at 2, 3 and 4
      players, with at least one win and one loss (`payment_made`, `session_end`).
- [ ] **Logs within 15 points of the sim:** `tools/sim/` `compare` on those logs; per dawn 2 to 8, live
      median coins as a share of the next payment vs the sim median (doc 02 s18.5).
- [ ] **Saving, joining and leaving:** one drop mid-season, one late join, one host leaving; debt
      recomputed per doc 01's example (4p, one drop before dawn 2: 1,135 total, first payment 223);
      resume from the last dawn save.
- [ ] **5 and 6 players:** at least one session at 5 or 6 (doc 01 "supported and scaled"); checks
      finding 1 above and voice bandwidth with 5 to 6 talkers.

### Phase 4 features, one check each

- [ ] Every crop plants, grows, sells at the town stand; the final dawn sells standing crops at half price.
- [ ] Prize Pumpkin: plant, water, guard, carry, judging at season end; `pumpkin_gnaw` bites it.
- [ ] Store: buy every item; upgrades seized on Foreclosure.
- [ ] Debt: payment dawns, early payment, Foreclosure when a payment is missed.
- [ ] Animals: escape through a `broken_fence`, round-up, the fee for an animal out at dusk.
- [ ] Roles: all ten pickable in the lobby, locked for the season, each perk noticed in play.
- [ ] Difficulty settings change the game as labeled.
- [ ] Short season and the Harvest Moon: festival cart on its route, the acts, the `harvest_moon` night.
- [ ] Walkie-talkies: bought, carried, heard with the radio sound.
- [ ] Season Awards and season end screen: every player gets at least one award.
- [ ] **Open Issue 4:** two sessions with different creature bodies (`--body=<id>`); can testers name
      the body by sound alone?
