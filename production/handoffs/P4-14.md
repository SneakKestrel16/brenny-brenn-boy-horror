## What was done

- **Walkies transmit per doc 06 s10.** A player holds a walkie when `Store.owns(peer, walkie_talkie)`
  and they are alive (D-013). Holding `voice_radio` (or `--radio-hold`) sets the radio flag; the host
  keeps it only through `Walkie.transmit()`, which checks the holder and drains 20 ms per frame. The
  relay carries the flag; clients play radio frames only on their own powered walkie (D-011), flat,
  on the `VoiceRadio` band-pass bus with the static layer. Proximity voice is unchanged.
- **Battery 180 s** from `store.json` `walkie_battery` `effect.transmit_s` (placeholder), 270 s for
  the Radio Operator (`roles.json` `battery_transmit_mult` 1.5, doc 01 "Roles"). The battery a walkie
  includes goes in at purchase; spares are a team pool loaded when a walkie runs flat (inference,
  Q-116). State goes out as `apply_walkie(peer, has_walkie, battery_s)`.
- **Radio sound per doc 08 s7.2** with P4-17's files: band-pass 500 Hz to 2.8 kHz,
  `vox_radio_static_loop` under each spurt at -20 dB rising to -6 dB as the creature comes from 30 m
  to 5 m of the listener, `vox_radio_squelch_on/off` per spurt, `vox_radio_low_battery` every 20 s at
  30 s or less (threshold placeholder), `vox_radio_dead` when the battery dies or a flat walkie is
  keyed. Not listened to (D-077: Dummy audio only); logs prove the path.
- **Q-065 confirmed** (peer-id applies stay; `apply_walkie` uses peer ids too). **Q-085** and
  **Q-102** answered: confirmed as built, no `net.gd` change. **Q-116** raised.
