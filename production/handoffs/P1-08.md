# P1-08 handoff: scripted creature (AI Programmer)

## What was done
- `game/creature/creature.gd`: host-only Creature (CharacterBody3D, group `creature`, layer 4, mask 1,
  placeholder dark capsule, body `body_gaunt`). Night script from `phase1.json` (doc 03 section 18):
  lurk 60 s, stalk the first outdoor player, chase at 15 s of stalk, retreat. After the script it
  hunts only by what it senses (doc 03 sections 3.1, 3.2): sound memory 12 s, louder wins within 4 s,
  short-range sight. Chase loss per doc 03 section 5 (commit 6 s, quiet 4 s, lit doorway 6 m via
  `Generator` fuel/damage and the `doors` group). Lurk wanders among cover and trap markers within
  25 m of the last thing it heard (a region, never a true position).
- Lures: generic stranger lines from corn perches `crow_01`, `_03`, `_06`, `_07`, `_09` (12 to 40 m
  from a lone target), logged as `lure_played` and `lure_result` with the doc 01 "Voice mimicry"
  rule (moved more than 10 m toward the source within 8 s). `moved_m` is logged unrounded so
  `check_logs.py` agrees with `worked`.
- Traps: 4 per night at 40/100/160/220 s (`phase1.json`), one bear and one pit across two trap spots;
  the 3rd and 4th set move the unsprung ones. `trap_changed` and `trap_sprung` (living player within
  1 m) are logged. The trap race itself is P1-09.
- Replication: state changes via `Net.apply_creature_state(state, body)` (reliable), body position
  via `send_bytes` type `0x03` at 15 Hz (18 bytes); clients lerp. `state_changed(state, body)` signal
  fires on host and clients for ambience hooks. Lures go to clients via `Net.apply_lure(...)`.
- `game/creature/creature_logic.gd`: pure rules (scripted timing, sound pick, lure distance), tested
  by `tests/creature/test_creature_logic.gd` (PASS).
- QA flags: `--creature-test` (night starts at once and repeats every `night_s`; a local walker loops
  the farm, follows 50% of heard lures, and the host walker steps on new traps); `--log-creature`
  (host `creature_debug` every 5 s; clients `creature_state_applied`).
