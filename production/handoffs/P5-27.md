  (night phases and generator powered), `prop_road_lamp` LightRig at its `LightRig` empty (night, own power),
  perched `animal_crow` `idle` at each `crow_perches` marker.

- **`game/creature/trap_art.gd`**: `tripwire(sprung)` (`trap_tripwire` / `trap_tripwire_sprung`); a set pit or bear gets
