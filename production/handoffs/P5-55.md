# P5-55 Closed doors and interactions (Gameplay Programmer)

## Cause (proven by running)
- **Players are NOT the leak.** `tests/qa/qa_p5_55_door_walk.gd` drives the real local Player (move_and_slide via `nav_path`) at the
  closed full-farm barn door: stopped at local z=-0.50 on the host AND on a client (two instances, ports 56801), and walks out once
  opened. `qa_p5_27_doors.gd` (test_move, all three doors) still passes. Door gap, models (3 m, doc 07) and `DoorBlock` agree.
  Standing in the doorway as it closes is depenetrated to the side the body was on (tested at z=-0.26), not through.
- **Bots were.** `bot.gd` moves by `_pos +=` (no physics) and in the full farm `_walk` makes a straight path, so bots crossed the
  barn wall and closed doors freely. With `--bot-chores` the bot left the closed barn at once (4062 leak frames in the test).
  (Inference: this is what the CEO saw; teammates are bots in solo/2-player sessions. A real second player was checked and is blocked.)
- Test traps: a teleported client is rubber-banded by the host (looks like walking through); `Game.local_peer()` is 1 on a client once the
  host quits. Both in docs/07_gotchas.md.

## Fix
`game/bots/bot.gd`: `_walk` routes a barn in/out crossing via `(0,0,-1.5)` and `(0,0,2)`; `_door_stops` stops a step across the barn
doorway when `door_barn` is closed and opens it (`Doors.host_set`, noise by the bot). Creature exception untouched. Doc 05 s11 updated.
