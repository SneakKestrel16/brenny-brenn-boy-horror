# P4-29 handoff: sprung bear traps stay to be hung back (AI Programmer)

Status: in review. CEO request 2026-10-09: "don't make the bear traps disappear after they are triggered,
make the players still interact with them after to put them back." Decision recorded as D-104.

## Built
- **Pry leaves the trap.** `TrapRace.on_pry_done` now marks the trap `loose` (logged `trap_changed`
  `{state: "loose", by, kind}`) and broadcasts `loose` instead of `disarmed`. Every peer drops the pry
  TrapTarget and the Creature shows the existing dawn-trap `TrapPickup` at the spot, jaws shut
  (`TrapArt.bear(true)`). The pinned-trap art also uses the shut jaws.
- **Pick up and hang.** Unchanged verbs: `take_trap` (instant) puts it in the hands (`picked_up`), then
  `hang_trap` on the pegboard (doc 02 s3, 1 s) fills an empty outline (`pegboard_changed` `hung`).
- **Nightfall (doc 01 "Any trap not on the pegboard at nightfall is the creature's").** `_steal_traps`
  takes loose traps after the hands and before the board (`trap_stolen` `from: "ground"`, trap
  `ground:<id>`). The order is an inference (D-104); a playtest settles it.
- The Creature's Phase-1 `_set_trap` skips spots that hold a loose trap.
- Unchanged: trap race, pry, slow-after, shaken, night theft from hands and board, bots (they pry
  themselves; they do not fetch traps).

## QA tooling fixes (pre-existing breakage found while verifying)
- `--force-spring` never fired: `TrapRace.traps` holds `set` traps too, so `not traps.has(id)` was always
  false. Now it skips only `sprung` ones. `sync_set` no longer re-sends `set` for a forced-sprung trap (it
  unpinned the victim mid-pry). The Creature clears `armed` on `loose`.
- `--autopry` reset `_scripted` every frame while unpinned, which cancelled any later scripted hold
  (`--take-loose`). It now resets once.
- `--take-loose` now also hangs the trap when an outline is empty (`_qa_take_and_hang` in creature.gd).
