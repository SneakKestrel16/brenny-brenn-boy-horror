## What was done

- **No absolute sanctuary anywhere.** The per-frame sanctuary retreat in chase, the sanctuary skips in
  the scripted stalk pick, lure, Harvest Moon knock-off, kill, and the trap-spot exclusions are gone.
  The trap-spot exclusion removal is a behavioural no-op: no `trap_spots` entry lies within 10 m of the
  stand at (120, 0, -5).
- **AI Director `stand_ok(kind, peer)`** (`game/ai_director/ai_director.gd`). If the player is within
  `town_stand.radius_m` of the stand (`creature._at_stand`, renamed from `_in_sanctuary`), it rolls
  `randf() < town_stand.<kind>_mult` once per kind and player and keeps the answer for `reroll_s`, so a
  creature does not re-roll every frame until it wins. Away from the stand it returns true. Seeded RNG
  (`Game.seed_value + 5`). Each roll logs `town_stand_roll {kind, player, won}`.
  `allow(kind, peer)` now ends with the stand roll for lure, scare, day_lure (rolled as lure), stalk and
  kill; chase is not rolled (the stalk pick already was). The old profile match moved to
  `_profile_allows`, so a roll is spent only when the profile would allow.
- **Creature** (`game/creature/creature.gd`, diff kept to the sanctuary sites for P4-25): scripted stalk
  pick needs `stand_ok(&"stalk")`; scripted and hunting kills call `allow(&"kill")`, emit `caught` only on a
  won roll, and retreat with reason `reached` or `town_stand`; scripted lures roll `stand_ok(&"lure")`;
  the Harvest Moon knock-off needs `stand_ok(&"knock_off")`. A creature that commits at the stand kills.
- **Kept:** a player at the stand counts as outside (`sabotage.gd _track_night` untouched). Bots keep the
  stand (sentinel) job; `bot.gd` changed in comments and the target string `"town_stand"` only.
- **Data** (`data/ai_director.json`, record `town_stand`, all placeholders, cite doc 01 "Town stand" /
  D-115): `radius_m` 10 (doc 01), `lure_mult` 0.25, `scare_mult` 0.25, `stalk_mult` 0.5,
  `knock_off_mult` 0.25, `kill_mult` 0.2, `reroll_s` 30. `scare_rules.sanctuary_m` removed; schema
  requires `town_stand`.
- **Docs:** doc 01 "Town stand" line (D-115 wording); doc 03 s9, s9.1, s11.5 (renamed "Private events,
  town stand", multiplier row), s11.8, s12.4, s13, s13.1, s14, s15; doc 04 stand references and s8.6
  title, svg label "town stand 10 m"; doc 05 debug circle name; doc 02 `sanctuary_m` row annotated.

Reading:

- **Economy matches P4-21.** Trample is 2 every night (base 1 + dead generator 1), as with the old
  sanctuary; P4-31's 5 is gone. Final shortfalls scatter both ways around P4-21 (6 better, 4 worse, 2
  equal). The scatter is bot-chore noise, not the stand: s3p_1's late first payment and its 1245 follow
  a day 3 foreclosure, with no death in that season before the Harvest Moon.
- **The guard never died.** 84 nights: about 45 stalk rolls won for the guard, 1 `kill` roll (s4p_2,
  lost at 0.2), 0 stand deaths. The creature rarely reaches the stand: it lurks about 100 m away, and
  the scripted night gives it 15 s stalk at 2.5 m/s plus 10 s chase at 5.5 m/s (about 92 m) before the
  retreat. Hunting after the script needs hearing or sight, both far below 100 m. So the multipliers do
  not set the guard's risk in these seasons; geography does. Raising `kill_mult` to 1 would give about 1
  death in 12 seasons and break "less likely". Multipliers left at their placeholders; Q-225 asks the
  Game Designer whether the creature should get reach to the stand.
- **Q-161 opinion:** the sim does not need a stand-guard death model now. The live risk is near zero,
  so the guard is a free "Unattended farm" = 0 and the sim's trample term is the open item, as before.
  If Q-225 gives the creature reach, add guard risk to the sim then, measured from re-run seasons.
