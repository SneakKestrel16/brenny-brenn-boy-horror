# P5-70 Door shut on the creature: it flings it open again (AI Programmer)

CEO D-188 on D-12 (production/DECISIONS.md): "the creature should fling the door open again if possible".

## Root cause

P5-27 exempts the creature from door collision, so only its walk keeps it out of a shut door. P5-55 opened a door
once per pass: once when the night bang ends (`_bang_open`), once on the way out (`_exit_open` guard in
`_bang_first`). A door a player shut after that one open stayed shut, and the creature walked through it. Day and
race entries (no bang) never opened the door at all.

## Fix

`game/creature/creature.gd`:
- New `_fling_doors(goal)`, called from `_move` each frame after the bang hold. For every building, when the creature
  is within `ARRIVE_M + 1.0` (2 m) of its door and is passing through it (its position and its goal are on different
  sides of the building rect), it calls the existing `_open_door(i)` (`Doors.host_set(..., true, 0)`; does nothing if
  already open). So any state, in or out, day or night, and again each time a player shuts it. No extra wait.
- The night entry bang is unchanged: `_bang_first` returns the step outside the door as the goal until the bang ends,
  so the "passing through" test is false until then.
- Removed the `_exit_open` once-per-exit guard (the new check covers exits).

No net change: same host path, same `apply_door` sync; doc 06 untouched.

Doc 03 section 6: added the "Shut doors (P5-70)" bullet after "Bang first".

## Test

`tests/creature/test_p5_70.gd` (new): a night chase into dark building 0. The test shuts the door every time it stands
open with the creature within 3 m (at most every 0.3 s), on the way in (right after the bang opens it) and on the way
out. Each frame it checks the creature is never within 0.6 m of the door point while the door is shut.
- New code: PASS (shut 2 times on the way in, 4 on the way out, 0 overlap frames).
- With the `_fling_doors` call disabled: FAIL (13 overlap frames in, 26 total).

Regression: parse_check, all tests/creature/*.gd, qa_p5_27_doors, qa_p5_55_leaf, qa_p5_55_bot_door,
qa_p5_55_door_walk (host only) all PASS; parse_check checked=231 failed=0; headless import no ERROR.

## Open

- The fling is silent (`by` 0: no door noise, the leaves snap open like a sync). A bang sound on a re-fling would make
  it more readable; it needs a design call (doc 03 owner), not built.
- A player holding the door shut gets it flung open every frame they shut it; there is no "if possible" case where it
  fails to open. Inference from D-188 "if possible": no lock exists in the game, so it is always possible.
