## Done

- Lobby toggle "Imposter mode (4+ players; ...)", host-editable, default off (D-158). Disabled when not host or fewer than 4 humans (D-161), and `Imposter.pick()` also ignores the toggle below 4 (Q-302).
- Pick at match start after `Roles.sync()`: toggle on, 4+ humans, `chance_pct` 50 (doc 01), one human at random. The secret goes to that peer alone (`apply_imposter_secret`). Nobody is told "no".
- The imposter keeps role and perks, never kills (`data/imposter.json`).
- Rejoin: `Net._admit` calls `Imposter.sync_to(id)`; the secret returns when the joiner's profile uid matches.
- Win only when the final payment is missed (`Debt.lost`, D-155). A first-payment Foreclosure Notice and a Harvest Moon wipe are not a win.
- Reveal line in the final Dawn Report and on the Season Awards card ("THE IMPOSTER WAS <name>. The farm was lost." / "The farm held." / "There was no imposter."). Shown only if the toggle was on or a dev pick forced one.
- Kit built: `whistle_throw` (key `[`), `gate_prop` (key `]`); `false_flag` is the imposter's normal flag. Host validates sender, ghost state, caps, cooldown; silent drop on refusal. Cut: `pegboard_mark`, `door_prop` (Q-303).
- Hidden dev setting: host console `imposter <peer id|name|me|none|off>`, gated by `DevGate.unlocked()` and host; otherwise answers like an unknown command; not in `help`. Forces the pick even below 4 players (Q-304).
- `imposter` registered in `game/core/data.gd` TABLES (P5-04 adds the same line; merge keeps one).
- Docs: `docs/05_technical_design.md` section 27 (renumbered at merge; P5-04 took 26) and two new log rows (`imposter_picked`, `imposter_action`). The `group_settings_imposter {enabled}` event is logged but has no table row (add if QA wants it).
- Secret never reaches other peers: not in the dawn save, no broadcast, host-only log events.
