1. Budget bypass: removed all role-timed silhouette, whisper, name_whisper, shadow and grab scares. 2. Own-voice: the Director's `own_voice` record (1 a season, `Game.replays_voice`, doc 06 s11). No local own-clip replay.
3. Fake grab: "turn" variant of the Director hallucination: distant silhouette (15-30 m, body glb), back to the player, turns after 2 s with `cre_jumpscare_hit` stinger, gone 1 s later. No camera jolt, so `camera_shake` no longer matters for the role.
4. Edge shadow: "edge" variant: the same silhouette placed 50-75 degrees off the facing (`shadow_edge_deg`), gone on 0.2 s of being looked at.

Variant picked by the host in `Scares.fire` (`extra` = "", "turn", "edge", 1/3 each, only for Horror targets), so it is host-authoritative and rides the existing `scare` message.
