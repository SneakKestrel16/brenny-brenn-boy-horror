## Fix round (text size)
Cause: `ThemeDB.fallback_font_size` is not what controls read. Fix: `SettingsApply._text_scale()` keeps one `Theme`, assigns it to `get_tree().root.theme`, and sets `default_font_size = roundi(16 * clamp(ui_text_scale, 0.8, 1.5))` at boot and on `ui_text_scale` change. `check_comfort.gd` no longer asserts the ThemeDB value. Doc 05 s16 updated.

Evidence (Comfort tab, 1280x720, `--menu --menu-open=settings --settings-tab=4 --ui-shot=...` with `ui_text_scale` set in settings.cfg; user settings.cfg restored afterwards): the "Toggle holds (press to start a hold, press again to stop)" line ends at x=446 at 0.8 (`ts_0.8.png`) and x=731 at 1.5 (`ts_1.5.png`); all other labels, tabs and the Back button scale too. Layout stays inside the panel at both extremes. No ERROR output.
