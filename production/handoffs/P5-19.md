# P5-19 handoff: `VoiceSplice.word_break` finds real silent frames

Owner: Network & Voice. Revised after the first QA FAIL (the cut landed in the VAD hangover tail).

## What changed

- `game/voice/splice.gd` `word_break`:
  - Noise floor = the 10th-percentile packet, `sorted[int(n * BREAK_FLOOR_PERCENTILE)]` with
    `BREAK_FLOOR_PERCENTILE := 0.1`, not the smallest packet. One tiny (DTX-like) packet can't lower it.
  - A frame is silent at `size <= max(1.15 x floor, 0.5 x median)`. `BREAK_FLOOR_SLACK := 1.15` was
    the Audio Designer's proposal. The old `BREAK_QUIET_SHARE` 0.5 x median rule stays as the floor.
  - The search runs only between the first and last loud frame. The leading and trailing quiet runs are
    trimmed first: they are the VAD pre-roll (`PREROLL_FRAMES` 5) and the hangover (`VAD_HANG_FRAMES`
    15 plus the `FLAG_TALK_END` frame, game/voice/voice.gd), not word gaps. `BREAK_EDGE_FRAMES` still
    applies as well.
  - No gap found: the cut is the middle of the loud span, `(first_loud + last_loud + 1) >> 1`, not
    `n >> 1`. A plain middle landed in the hangover on short clips: n=25 cut at 12 with the tail
    starting at 9 (second QA FAIL).
  - Uniform guard: if the floor is at least half the largest packet (`BREAK_SPREAD := 0.5`), the clip has no
    silence to find (all speech, all silence, one size). The cut is then mid-clip. The check uses `>=`,
    so a clip of empty packets also stops there and the loud-frame scan can't run out of range. A
    test covers this.
- `tests/net/test_splice.gd`:
  - The first gap clip gained quiet 5-frame ends, because the percentile floor needs over 10% quiet
    frames. Real VAD clips always have them.
  - Measured-sizes clip, 60 frames: silence 24-26, speech 40-70, median asserted at 40-43. Cut at 42.
  - Uniform guard: all 24, all 50, all silent 24/26, and all speech with a quieter syllable. Each cuts
    mid-clip (20).
  - New `_vad_clip` helper: 5-frame pre-roll, 16-frame hangover tail, speech 40-70 in between, 80 frames.
    - VAD-shaped clip with a 3-frame interior gap at 30..32: cut at 31.
    - Edge silence only: the middle of speech 5..63, which is 34.
    - VAD clips of n = 25..32 frames (from `CLIP_MIN_FRAMES` 25) with no gap: every cut is >= 5 and
      < n-16.
    - VAD clip with one 3-byte packet at frame 20 plus the real gap: cut still at 31.
- `docs/06_networking_and_voice.md` section 12, the "Word break (P5-19)" bullet:
  - The search runs between the first and last loud frame.
  - It names the VAD pre-roll and hangover constants, the percentile floor, the measured sizes, and the
    microphone-floor inference.
- I reverted my earlier `production/TASKS.md` status edit. The row is back to `in progress`.

## Numbers

- Threshold on measured sizes: max(24 x 1.15, ~41 x 0.5) = 27.6 bytes. Silent frames (24-26) pass.
  The smallest speech frame (40) does not.
- The percentile floor holds while quiet frames are over 10% of the clip. A clip ended by TALK_END
  carries at least 21 quiet frames (5 + 16), which is over 10% up to the 150-frame cap. A clip cut at
  the cap without TALK_END may have a speech-level floor. The spread guard then gives a mid-clip
  cut, as with `live_1`.

## Q-291 answer

Q-291 is not in `production/QUESTIONS.md` in this worktree or the main checkout, so I worked from the
task brief and the QA notes. Answer: adopted the proposal, with three changes:

- The floor is the 10th-percentile packet, not the smallest.
- The search is trimmed to between the first and last loud frame, so the VAD hangover is never the
  break.
- A spread guard (floor <= 0.5 x largest) cuts uniform clips mid-clip.
