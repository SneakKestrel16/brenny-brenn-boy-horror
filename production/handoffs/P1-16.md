# P1-16 Gameplay: STOP 2 readiness (Q-046)

## Done
1. **Stamina flicker.** Root cause was two things. (a) `sprinting` flipped every frame at stamina 0:
   fixed with `exhausted` hysteresis in `game/player/player.gd` (no sprint again until stamina
   reaches `RESUME_S` = 1.5 s, placeholder; doc 02 s2.2 gives only max and refill). (b) The packet
   carried the flag at send time, so a sprint-to-walk change inside a 50 ms interval read as 3.6 to
   4.3 m/s against a walk limit of 3.0 (this also hits any human who releases Shift). Now the client
   sends sprint = "sprinted at any time this interval" and crouch = "crouched the whole interval".
   Measured, 2 instances, 1x, `--phase1 -- --autowalk --autosprint` (sprint held, new QA flag), 5400
   frames: 0 `speed_violation` on host and client (before the interval fix: 12 in 90 s; before any fix: ~548 per 660 s).
2. **HUD** `game/ui/hud.gd` (CanvasLayer, local player, built by `Player._ready`): top-left
   day, phase, time left, coins (+ Shaken seconds); bottom-centre prompt "Hold E: <verb>" for the
   aimed holdable and "<verb>... N%" while holding; stamina bar (red while exhausted); a controls
   hint for 20 s from spawn (keys read from InputMap); top banner for dead/ghost and for pinned in a
   trap. Text only. `HoldController` got `aimed_verb` and `hold_state()`. Windowed screenshot
   looked at (`logs/shots/hud.png`): hint, clock, coins, bar render. The aimed prompt was not
   screenshotted (the shot camera is separate from the player ray); it ran in the `--autochore` run
   with no errors. Needs a human look.
3. **`inside_at_night`**: `game/player/players.gd`, host, one per player at dawn, `player` and
   `seconds` (living players inside the Barn or ToolShed floor rect, hard-coded from `build_farm.py`).
   Seen in a 2-instance `--time-scale 10 --autochore` run (300.2 s each, both bots stood in the barn).
4. **`shaken` on the client**: `trap_race.gd` writes `shaken {player, seconds}` on a non-host peer
   when it applies the host's Shaken. No new RPC.
