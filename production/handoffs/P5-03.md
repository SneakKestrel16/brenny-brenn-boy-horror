## Wire format

`apply_lure(lure_id, source, position, target_slot, tell, ghost)`, unchanged shape. `source`:

- exact clip: `clip:<owner_peer>:<clip_id>` (as before);
- splice: `clip:<owner_peer>:<clip_id>@<first>+<count>,<clip_id>@<first>+<count>`, e.g.
  `clip:1:live_1@0+75,live_0@30+31`.

Clip ids are `[A-Za-z0-9_]` (VoiceClips `_valid_id`), so `@`, `+` and `,` never collide. Parse
refuses: empty, more than 2 segments, negative first, zero count, bad id, missing `+`.
`lure_played` gains `exact` (false for a splice) and `segments` (`[[clip_id, first, count]]`, one
whole-clip segment for an exact clip lure, null for stranger and sound lures).
