# P5-72 handoff: creature AI fixes from the outside review (AI Programmer)

The CEO passed on an outside review of the creature AI (2026-10-11). This pass checks sight, adds
stuck recovery to every state, routes a target-less stalk through the corn and gives a lost stalk the
same search as a lost chase. Hunting still uses only sensed positions (doc 01 "The AI Director",
D-189). Pacing is out of scope; ideas are at the end.

## 1. Line of sight: already fine, now proven

- `_sense()` casts eye (1.65 m) to head with mask `1 | 16`. Players are on layer 2, the creature on 4
  and interactables on 8, so none of them is in the mask. Target exclusion is not needed, and none was
  added.
- `test_p5_72` part A lists every layer 1/16 body on the full farm above the 0.9 m crouched eye,
  leaving out buildings, bounds, trees, corn blockers and doors. The list holds Generator (1.2 m), the
  fuel drum, well, shipping crate and town stand (1.0 m), the eight pen fence sections (1.2 m) and
  GatePostN/S (2.0 m). All are below the eye line. The gate posts are thin posts at the farm gate, so
  hiding directly behind one is fair.
- A player 1.2 m behind each prop and two pen fence sections is seen from 5 m. A player inside the
  barn is not seen from 4 m outside (the ray hits `Barn/WallE`).
- The probe found one more occluder: the tool shed's open door leaf (`ToolShed/DoorArt/LeafR/LeafBody`,
  layer 1) blocks a ray 5 m west of the fuel drum. A 2 m door is a real occluder, so it was left alone.
- Doc 03 s3.2 now records this.

## 2. Stuck recovery in every state (fixed)

Before, the stuck check ran only in lurk. A chase into the pen fence pushed against it: in the old
code, test B got from 21.0 m to 18.5 m in 20 s and logged no `creature_stuck`. In the new code, any
walk that is stuck for `STUCK_S` (2 s) calls `_unstick()`:

- It walks sideways along the obstacle, at most `stuck_step_m` (new, placeholder 8 m).
- The step ends once a chest-height ray (mask 1) to the goal clears, after at least 1 m.
- If it is stuck again during the step, it turns back. A full step that does not clear sends the next
  stuck to the other side.
- What each state does on top of the side step:
  - lurk drops its route (as before);
  - stalk picks a new hiding spot at least 3 m from the one it could not reach (`_hold_bad`);
  - retreat picks a new point;
  - chase re-heads for the sensed position from the new side.
- Holds do not count. Each of these returns before the stuck tail of `_move`, and any frame without a
  walk restarts the clock:
  - the bang hold;
  - the P5-69 hide wait;
  - the stalk hold or standoff stop, and the Harvest Moon standoff (a stalk stopping 10 m off).
- A chase standing within reach + 1 m of its goal does not count either. This covers the act 2 lunge
  and the bite at the cart.
- Trap setting places the trap at once, with no walk to hold, so it cannot trip the check.
- `creature_stuck` now logs `state`.
- In the new code, test B logs 1 chase stuck and comes within 1.4 m of the target in 7.9 s.
- `ponytail:` the side step is blind, since there is no navmesh. It works for fences and props. An
  obstacle longer than twice `stuck_step_m` would still trap it.

## 3. Stalk toward cover

- (a) The scripted stalk is unchanged.
- (b) Fixed: a target==0 stalk on a noise (for example a dead generator) now walks a corn route
  (`_route_to`, which reuses `Logic.corn_route`). Test C, from cover_10 to a noise at cover_01:
  - old code: 72 open metres, a straight line;
  - new code: 48 open metres, 4 hops.
- (c) Left as built: Harvest Moon acts 2-3 walk straight. This is by design. Doc 03 s14 says it
  "attacks the pusher it senses loudest", and P5-56 found that corn routes kept it off the cart.

## 4. Search after a lost stalk (fixed)

- `_lose()` is shared by a lost chase and a `lost_track` stalk. It sets the search for `search_s`
  (new, placeholder 12 s = `hearing_memory_s`) round the last **sensed** position, using the same
  cover-point search as P5-56.
- Test D:
  - It searches for 12 s round the sensed position (51, 0, -6), not the true position (-59, 0, -60).
  - It then walks to a cover point 4.5 m away, within `search_radius_m`.
  - In the old code, `_search_c` stayed INF and `search_s` did not exist.
