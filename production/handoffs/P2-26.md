## Causes
1. **Barn.** Without Phase 1 data the host creature is idle (`_ok` false, "needs --phase1" warning) and was
   never moved: `main.gd` adds it at the origin, which is the barn's south wall and door (barn x -8..8,
   z -20..0, `farm.tscn`). A client also drew it at the origin until the first `creature` packet
   (`_target_pos` started at `Vector3.ZERO`). With P2-24's data default the host now runs it, so the
   CEO's case no longer happens by default. The idle origin placement was still a bug.
2. **Traps.** Same root: an idle creature sets no traps, so there was nothing to draw. With the data on,
   set bear traps and pits reach the client and draw there (evidence below). The P1-20 clue meshes
   were fine. They are seen only within 4 m by design (doc 03 s9 "Clues", doc 01 "Night Traps":
   "visible only to players who look closely").
