# P5-39 handoff: better creature AI (AI Programmer)

CEO asked "can you improve the creatures ai" and called it "pretty bad". This pass covers Q-345 (1), (3)
and (4). It does not touch Q-345 (2), the day full-body visibility point, which is a CEO call.
Hunting still uses only sensed positions (doc 03 section 3); no fix reads a true player position.

## Problems, ranked by how bad they look to a watcher

Measured on headless bot seasons, seeds 1-3, `--fixed-fps 60`, one `creature_debug` sample per 5 s of
night (logs under `logs/ai_improve/`, scripts `season.sh`, `stats.awk`, `sum.sh`, `loops.sh`).

1. **Parks in the open for whole nights.** With no sense it walked to the `town_road` region centre and
   stood there (`goal_null` samples, `stuck` samples). Fix: lurk wanders between cover points at
   least `wander_min_hop_m` apart, with a region fallback, and never re-picks the spot it stands on.
2. **Ignores a silent player in plain sight.** Lurk only reacted to sound; a crouched player 10 m away
   in view was never stalked (`lurk_seen`). Fix: lurk also stalks on fresh sight (still gated by the
   AI Director's `allow(&"stalk")`).
3. **Stalk walks straight at you and stops 10 m off in the open.** Fix: stalk picks a hiding spot on a
   ring around the sensed target, nearest point the target cannot see (world plus corn occlude, not in
   a lit doorway). The ring closes from `stalk_hold_far_m` 18 m to `stalk_hold_near_m` 9 m as
   `stalk_max_s` runs out; a new spot every `stalk_repick_s` 6 s. Fallback: a 45 degree flank, side
   chosen at random per stalk.
4. **Gives up a stalk and re-stalks the same player in the same second; walks onto their old spot.**
   Fix: on `stalk_max_s` it clears memory, drops its goal and leaves that player alone for
   `stalk_rest_s` 30 s.
5. **Retreat always runs to the single farthest cover point.** Fix: a random cover point at least
   `retreat_min_m` 30 m off and farther from the target than from the creature; farthest as fallback.
6. **Silent kill inside a dark building.** Doc 03 section 6 says it bangs first. Found in the first
   pass of this task (2 `night_chase` deaths in the dark Barn on seed 2, no bang), not in the base
   build. Fix: before entering a dark building at night it stops at the door step for `door_bang_s`
   3 s and sends `sound:door_fake` (every peer hears `cre_door_bang`) once a second; no catch through
   the door while banging. Not applied in daylight, in a trap race, or when the buildings are lit
   (`_shut_out` already keeps it out then).

Q-345 (1), (3) and (4): see the earlier AI-IMPROVE-01 notes in Q-347 (trap race and hold registry edits).
