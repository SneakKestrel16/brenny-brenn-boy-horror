## Built
`game/items/store.gd` (class `Store`, child of `Farm` on every peer). Host-authoritative: `request_store(op, arg)` ->
validate -> mutate -> log -> `apply_store(state)` to all (late joiners get it on `farm_state`). See doc 05 section 9
"The store".

- Every `store.json` row buys at its price and `unlock_day`, within 3.5 m of the `store_crate` (placeholder). Refusals:
  `too_far`, `locked_item`, `no_coins`, `owned`, `max_bought`, `plots_max`, `ghost`, `no_item`. No crop is sold; seeds
  are charged at planting (P4-04).
- `store_buy` logs item, price, buyer, day, coins. Also `store_refused`, `store_picked`, `scrap_used`, `flare_fired`,
  `scarecrow_placed`, `store_seized` (doc 05 s18).
- `scrap`: `take_scrap()` spends the free scrap first, then bought. Free scrap does not stack (existing dawn step).
- `quiet_watering_can` (per player): verb `water_quiet`, noise x0.5.
- `brighter_lantern`: tracked, `lantern_mult(peer)` 1.5.
- `shed_lock`: sets `creature.shed_lock`.
- `scarecrow` (max 3): placed at the player's feet (key N), 3 m apart; creature avoids by `creature_avoid_m` 10.
- `flare_gun`: one shot (key H), `noise_flare`, creature within 70 m goes to Retreat 30 s; refilled in dawn step 7.
- `plot_pair`: unlocks 2 locked field plots, refused past `farm.plot_ceiling()`.
- `walkie_talkie` / `walkie_battery`: stocked (talkie adds its included battery); P4-14 builds the radio.
- P4-07 hooks: `Store.seizable()` and `Store.seize(id)`.
- Keys: R cycle item, K buy, H flare, N scarecrow. Dev console `buy <item>`.
