### speed_violation under `--autowalk` (OPEN_ISSUES)
- Owner: **Gameplay** (`game/player/player.gd` stamina, read by `game/player/players.gd` check). Not network, not bots, not the check.
- Evidence: it also fires for the **host's own player** (no network hop; seen in the windowed probe run), at speeds 3.6 to 4.3 against max 3.0 (limit 3.0 x 1.2 = 3.6). `--autochore` (sprint forced off) at 1x: 0 in 900 s. Violations come in bursts, about every 8 s, matching autowalk's 4 s sprint half-cycle.
- Root cause (inference, strong): when `stamina` hits 0 with sprint held, `sprinting` flips every frame (regen lifts `stamina` above 0, one sprint frame drains it), so the body moves between 3.0 and 5.0 m/s while the packet's `sprint` flag is mostly false and the host judges it as walk. Same comment already sits in `--autochore` ("a stamina-flipping sprint trips the host speed check").
- Consequence for humans: holding Shift past 6 s (`labor.json` sprint `max_s`) should log violations and let the host clamp the player. Not yet shown with a human. Settle: log `stamina` and `sprinting` in the event, or hold Shift 10 s in a windowed run with the log open. Fix is a refill threshold in `player.gd`. Q-046 item 1.
