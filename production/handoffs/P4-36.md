**Role:** 3D Artist. **Status:** in review.

## What was done

Ten low-poly role hats, one per role id in `data/roles.json`, built by script and exported as glb.
Each hat is one mesh object, `Hat`, with vertex colour on `mat_flat_lit`. None of it glows (doc 07 s2).

## For Gameplay: loading in `LineUp._hat` (lobby.gd)

- **Path:** `res://assets/models/hat_%s.glb % role`, where `role` is the role id StringName.
- **Origin:** the centre of the band's bottom edge, where the hat meets the head. Place the instanced
  scene at `position.y = 1.74` on the farmer root, x = z = 0. That fits the lobby head sphere (r 0.16,
  centre 1.62). Parts below the origin (ear flaps, headphone cups, boom mic) clear that sphere.
- **Scale:** 1. Real metres. No rotation: +Y up, front is -Z like the farmer.
- The P4-35 branch's `_hat(role)` (load if `ResourceLoader.exists`, else placeholder, then `position.y = 1.74`)
  already matches this; no change needed beyond that.
- For the later in-game `char_farmer.glb`, parent the hat to a head bone or attachment at the top of the
  head band (about 1.74 m in rest pose).

Findings:

1. **In the lobby every hat faces away from the camera.** The glbs face -Z (D-144, handoff "Scale"), as do
   the farmers, but the lobby camera sits at +Z looking -Z (`game/ui/lobby.gd:221`, `cam.position =
   Vector3(0, 1.6, 8.2)`). `LineUp._farmer` (`lobby.gd:85`) does not rotate the farmer, and `_hat`
   (`lobby.gd:138-143`) does not rotate the hat. So the camera sees each hat's back. The medic cross, the
   mechanic goggles, the night owl headlamp, the warden badge, the medium crescent and the radio boom mic
   are all hidden. The mechanic's backwards peak points at the camera, so it reads as a cap worn forwards.
   Compare `qa_noflip_zoomC.png` (night owl, no lamp) with `qa_flip_zoomC.png` (lamp shows), and
   `qa_a_zoomR.png` (medic, no cross) with `qa_flip_zoomL.png` / `qa_flip_zoomR.png` (goggles, boom mic and
   crescent show). Fix (Gameplay, `lobby.gd`): turn each farmer 180 degrees in `_farmer`
   (`f.rotation.y = PI`), so a later `char_farmer.glb` faces the camera as well. Turning only the hat in
   `_hat` also works. The assets are correct as delivered. Filed as Q-242.
2. **The night owl tufts read as cat ears.** They are two upright 4-sided cones
   (`tools/blender/build_phase4.py:768-769`). In the agent's `hats_close_a_front.png` and in the lobby
   (`qa_noflip_zoomC.png`), they look like cat ears, not owl tufts. At line-up distance (`qa_b_full.png`),
   they read as a white cat-eared beanie. Suggested change (3D Artist): use the handoff's fallback pompom, or
   flatten the tufts and tilt them outward with feathered tips. The CEO should look before anyone rebuilds,
   because cat ears may be acceptable. Not blocking.
3. **The centre spot washes out the local player's hat colour.** Under the 14-energy spot
   (`game/ui/lobby.tscn:16`), the navy night owl beanie renders near-white (`qa_b_full.png`,
   `qa_noflip_zoomC.png`), and the straw farmer hat looks white (`qa_a_full.png`). Colour is the second
   cue after silhouette (handoff table). This belongs to the lobby lighting (Gameplay/Technical Artist), not
   to the hats. Not blocking.
4. **At full line-up scale, hats are about 25-35 px wide** (`qa_a_full.png`, `qa_b_full.png`). The
   silhouettes still differ: wide brims, cone, antenna, ears, box. Up close they are clearly distinct.
   They sit on the heads: each crown covers the head top, none floats, and none sinks past the band
   (`qa_a_side.png`, the zoom shots). No action needed.
