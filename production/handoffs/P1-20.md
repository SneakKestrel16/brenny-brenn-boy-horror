# P1-20 handoff: playtest issues 6, 7, 8 (AI Programmer)

Task given by the Director after the Phase 1 playtest (`production/OPEN_ISSUES.md`, "Found at the
Phase 1 playtest", items 6 to 8). No TASKS.md row exists yet; the id is the next free P1 number.

## What was done

All in `game/creature/creature.gd`.

1. **Traps visible (issue 6).** Cause: by design, a set trap stayed host-only (`net.gd` comment "Set
   traps are never sent") and `TrapRace` draws only sprung traps. Now `_set_trap` sends
   `apply_trap_changed(id, kind, &"set", pos)` to every client, and `moved` for the old spot when the
   third or fourth set time moves a trap. Every peer draws a clue under the `trap_spots` marker
   (`_show_clue`): bear trap = metal disc r 0.35 m with faint emission ("glinting metal", doc 01 Night
   Traps), pit = dirt disc r 0.8 m. `visibility_range_end` 4 m (doc 03 s9 `CLUE_M`). Any other state
   (sprung, disarmed, moved) removes it; `TrapRace` then draws the sprung trap as before. A late joiner
   gets the armed traps from the `farm_state` handler.
2. **Lures (issue 7).** Cause (inference from the host log, settled by a playtest with this build):
   lures ran only in the unscripted lurk and only for a lone player (no living player within 15 m).
   The session's nights were scripted for most of their length and the three players stayed together.
   Now `_try_lure` runs in the scripted lurk too, and `_play_lure` lets a non-lone player be lured from
   a crow perch within `TRAP_LURE_M` 15 m of an armed trap (doc 01 "Lure: near armed traps or lone
   players"). A lure in the scripted lurk returns to lurk after `_track_lure`, so the script keeps its
   stalk time. The source choice reads the target's true position and the perch markers only; the corn
   being walkable or not does not matter.
3. **Death (issue 8).** Log `logs/qa/stop1_host/20261007_225806_31fe/peer_1.jsonl`: scripted stalk at
   t 660, the creature held 10 m (`STALK_STANDOFF_M`) from the host for 15 s, inside `sight_night_m`
   15 m, chased at 675.09 from about 8 m, death at 676.27 (1.18 s). The host was the victim, so lag
   was not involved. The other 4 chases ended `lit_building` once and `lost` three times. This breaks
   doc 03 s2 (never in clear view at night except mid-chase) and the s4 chase tell (music sting plus
   signature: neither exists). Fix: scripted stalk holds `SCRIPTED_STANDOFF_M` 18 m and backs off when
   the target walks closer; no catch before `CHASE_TELL_S` 2 s into a chase. New log fields:
   `chase_started.reason`, `chase_started.start_m` (true distance, log only, hunting never reads it),
   `chase_ended.chase_s`.
