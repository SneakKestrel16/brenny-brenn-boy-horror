Status: in review. Committed in the worktree, not pushed. `sim.py compare` prints FAIL (12 checks out of range).
The other acceptance lines pass: 2 wins and 11 losses, 0 night deaths, 0 `speed_violation` at time-scale 8, and bots use only player paths.

## Built (`game/bots/bot.gd` only)
- **Night (doc 03 s6, s10, s11.5, s18).** At dusk, bots sell their bag and go into the barn (`SHELTER_SPOT`), entering by the door. They come out at dawn.
  - Short trips outdoors happen only if the bot is back inside by `HOME_BY_S` 50 s. This is before the scripted 60 s stalk. Trips are moonflower harvest/tend, generator repair at dusk, and fuel-can prep and refuel.
  - One bot is the **sentinel** (the highest living bot peer). From dusk to dawn it stands 4 m inside the town-stand sanctuary (farm.tscn `Sanctuary`, x 120). That bot is outdoors all night, so `nobody_outside_s` is 0. The scripted stalk picks it, and the chase ends without a kill.
  - Result: dawn trample drops from 5 to 2 plots a night (base 1 + dead generator 1). See Q-161 for the design question this raises.
- **Keep the first payment (doc 02 s7).** `_keeps_payment` buys a seed whose crop ripens after the first-payment dawn only from coins above that dawn's payment.
- **Prize Pumpkin.** Bots plant it and water it daily. At the Harvest Moon a bot drops its can, returns the shovel (`return_shovel`, hands must be free) and then lifts. Only the bot holding the `prize_pumpkin` claim lifts; see Q-163 for the double-lift bug.
- **Trap race.** A pinned bot holds `pry` on its trap after 0.3 s. All bot trap races were survived.
- **Speed.** Bots move only on send steps (`_step`), so the host speed check sees the same distance per frame as a client.
- Wilted or dead plots get `clear_plot` so planting can continue.
- Every action goes through `players.submit` and `Net.request_received.emit(&"hold", ...)`. There are no host shortcuts. The one exception is the existing D-085 `store.buy(... near=false)` for scrap.

Findings:
1. **Moonflower seeds escape the payment reserve** (`game/bots/bot.gd:286`). Moonflower `grow_days` is 0
   (data/crops.json), so on day 3 the test `Clock.day + 0 < first_payment_dawn 4` is true and the bot buys
   moonflowers from the payment money. A moonflower sells only if a bot harvests it at night. At 2p the
   sole bot is the sentinel, so nobody does. Keeping those coins would not save either 2p payment (both
   banks stay below the 150 due). It does turn a small shortfall into "every coin on seeds", which the
   acceptance forbids. Smallest fix: count a moonflower as ripening after the payment dawn, or skip
   moonflowers when no non-sentinel bot is alive.
2. **The sentinel exploits a rule; it is not median-team behaviour** (`bot.gd:254`, Q-161). Doc 01
   "Nights": "Hiding is never fully safe or free". Doc 01 "Sanctuary": no lures, scares or kills within
   10 m of the town stand. `game/ai_director/sabotage.gd:331` `_track_night` counts any outdoor player, so
   one player parked at the town stand (x 120, off the farm) cancels the whole "Unattended farm" term at no
   risk. The lever is 3 plots a night: trample 5 in every v2 night, 2 in every final night. Sales in
   s3p_1 went from +230 to +800. A human team could find this, so it is a real rule hole. Bot seasons that
   use it measure the hole, not a median team: their economy numbers are an upper bound. Night deaths do
   not depend on it: v2 (shelter, no sentinel) also had 0 night deaths. Filed as Q-164.
3. **The acceptance says "inside a lit building"**, but the barn is dark for most of every night.
   `generator_dead` fires in 7 of 7 nights of every season, and bots never go out to refuel after
   `HOME_BY_S` 50 s. The QA `qts8_3p` night death shows the cost: bot -2 fell in a pit on a moonflower
   trip, was Tainted, did not wash (`next_job` skips `wash` at night, `bot.gd:146`), waited in the dark
   barn and was chased down there. Doc 01 "Nights": "Fuel runs low mid-night ... the walk is the cost".
   Inference: a median team refuels. Human seasons would settle it.
4. **Harvest Moon deaths.** The sim spreads the median's 2 deaths over nights 1 to 7 (`sim.py:188`,
   `nights = range(1, M.days + 1)`), and night 7 is the Harvest Moon. Counting `harvest_moon` deaths,
   `s4p_2`, `short4p_2`, `ts8_4p` and QA `qts8_3p` have 3 deaths each, above 2. Line 1 passes only if
   "night deaths" means nights 1 to 6. The Director should rule (Q-164).
5. `_pry` (`bot.gd:85`) tries once per trap (`_pin_t = -1000`). If the pry hold is refused or cancelled,
   the bot stays pinned until it dies. It matches `hold_controller.gd` `--autopry`. Low risk, not blocking.
6. Code reads like the rest of `bot.gd`: same comment density, `ponytail:` notes on real corners, no dead
   code. The speed fix is correct: `_step` walks `walk * speed_mult * ticks / SEND_HZ` along the path (so
   the straight-line move is never longer), and `seq` grows by the same `ticks`. `players.gd:177` then
   reads `dt >= ticks / SEND_HZ`, so the frame is at most `max_speed`. The `shelter` branch in `_run`
   repeats `_walk`'s wait loop (`bot.gd:124`); acceptable.
7. Process: the P4-21 row in TASKS.md still says `in progress`, not `in review`.
