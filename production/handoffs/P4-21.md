## Built (`game/bots/bot.gd` only)
- **Night (doc 03 s6, s10, s11.5, s18).** At dusk, bots sell their bag and go into the barn (`SHELTER_SPOT`), entering by the door. They come out at dawn.
  - Short trips outdoors happen only if the bot is back inside by `HOME_BY_S` 50 s. This is before the scripted 60 s stalk. Trips are moonflower harvest/tend, generator repair at dusk, and fuel-can prep and refuel.
  - One bot is the **sentinel** (the highest living bot peer). From dusk to dawn it stands 4 m inside the town-stand sanctuary (farm.tscn `Sanctuary`, x 120). That bot is outdoors all night, so `nobody_outside_s` is 0. The scripted stalk picks it, and the chase ends without a kill.
  - Result: dawn trample drops from 5 to 2 plots a night (base 1 + dead generator 1). See Q-161 for the design question this raises.
- **Keep the first payment (doc 02 s7).** `_keeps_payment` buys a seed whose crop ripens after the first-payment dawn only from coins above that dawn's payment.
- **Prize Pumpkin.** Bots plant it and water it daily. At the Harvest Moon a bot drops its can, returns the shovel (`return_shovel`, hands must be free) and then lifts. Only the bot holding the `prize_pumpkin` claim lifts; see Q-163 for the double-lift bug.
- **Trap race.** A pinned bot holds `pry` on its trap after 0.3 s. All bot trap races were survived.
- **Speed.** Bots move only on send steps (`_step`), so the host speed check sees the same distance per frame as a client.
- Wilted or dead plots get `clear_plot` so planting can continue.
- Every action goes through `players.submit` and `Net.request_received.emit(&"hold", ...)`. There are no host shortcuts. The one exception is the existing D-085 `store.buy(... near=false)` for scrap.
