# P4-26 handoff: new footsteps per surface, sparser crickets

Audio Designer. Source: TASKS "P4-22 to P4-28 CEO session fixes" (P4-26), OPEN_ISSUES "Found in the CEO's
2-instance session" item 8. The CEO said the footsteps sound bad and the crickets chirp too often.

## What was done

- **New footstep set, 6 variants per surface** (doc 08 s11.2): `sfx_step_dirt_01..06`, `sfx_step_corn_01..06`,
  and the new `sfx_step_wood_01..06` (doc 08 listed wood; it was never built). Noise only, no oscillator:
  the old P1-10 steps had a sine "boop" body, the likely reason they sounded synthetic. Each step is a heel
  thud, a toe roll 60 to 120 ms later, a scuff and surface texture (soil grains; corn adds leaf brush and
  dry-leaf crackle; wood is a noise-excited damped plank `Klank` with straw grit and a faint creak on even
  variants). Each variant seeds its own timing, weight, tone and grit, so variants differ in more than pitch.
  Sources are generated by `tools/audio/gen_steps.py` (edit the recipe there, not the 18 `.scd` files).
  Rendered RMS: dirt -17.3 to -20.1, corn -17.3 to -21.2, wood -19.8 to -24.4 dBFS; peaks -1 dBFS.
- **Per-step variation** (`game/audio/soundscape.gd`): a random variant, never the one just played (also
  for every other multi-variant sound), pitch 0.9 to 1.1, level -2.5 to +1 dB per step. `play_2d` got an
  optional `pitch` argument; `play_3d` multiplies `opts.pitch`.
- **Surface pick**: corn if the step is inside a corn area (layer 5 point query), wood if inside a building
  footprint (built once per scene from the `doors` markers' buildings' wall bodies), else dirt. Before this,
  only corn and dirt existed.
- **Sprint threshold fix**: the sprint level (+6 dB) was gated on speed `> 5.0`, but sprint speed is exactly
  5.0 (`data/labor.json`), so sprinting mostly played walk-level steps. It now uses the midpoint of walk and
  sprint speed.
- **Crickets** (`amb_insect_bed_night`, doc 08 s11.1): each of the 20 voices now chirps every 3.0 to 5.5 s
  (was 0.9 to 1.6 s): about 4.7 trains a second across the bed instead of 16. The high-noise floor is
  unchanged, so the bed never goes silent and the Stalk drop stays audible.
- Footstep Noise and all gameplay are unchanged: the host's Noise comes from `move` frames (players.gd),
  which this task didn't touch.
- Doc 08 updated: s3.2 (per-step variation and surface pick), s11.1 insect bed row, s11.2 step rows.
