# P5-68 handoff: the jumpscare shows the creature body

The jumpscare now shows this season's body model (gaunt, scarecrow, boar or husk) instead of the placeholder capsule. Doc 03 s13.1 has the full rules. Doc 01 "What players see" says "Stares and hallucinations: distant silhouettes". Following that, hallucinations now show the body as a black silhouette. The wrong count stays a brown farmer capsule, because it shows a farmer, not the creature.

## What changed

- `game/creature/creature.gd`: new `static func body_path(b)`, the one body-to-glb lookup. `_show_body` and Scares both use it. body_husk maps to `creature_corn_husk.glb`.
- `game/ai_director/scares.gd`:
  - `_apparition(pos, kind, seconds)` gets its model from `_body_art`. Jumpscares get the body with `CreatureLook` materials. Hallucinations get the body as an unshaded near-black silhouette.
  - The apparition turns to face the camera.
  - Jumpscare framing:
    - The body is pushed back by its own front extent (from the mesh AABBs), so its front sits `JUMP_GAP_M` 0.9 m from the player. The boar's snout no longer clips the camera.
    - A body shorter than eye height plus `JUMP_OVER_EYE_M` 0.3 m is raised as if mid-leap, so its face fills the view. Only the boar is short enough.
    - An `OmniLight3D` (range 4 m, default energy) sits between the face and the camera, so the body reads at night. grep_rules rule 3 forbids `light_energy` outside game/render, so the default energy stays.
  - The capsule appears only when the body is unknown or its glb is missing.
  - Header comment updated.
- `docs/03_creature_ai_director_and_scares.md` s13.1 "Presentation" now describes the body, the silhouette and the capsule fallback.

All new numbers are placeholders tuned by eye from the screenshots below. None comes from doc 01.
