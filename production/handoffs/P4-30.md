# P4-30 handoff: headcount sell bonus (Game Designer)

## What was done
- **Mechanism.** `data/player_scaling.json` `sell_bonus_pct_by_players` (keys 2 to 6, integer %, `sim`;
  schema in `data/player_scaling.schema.json` and doc 02 A.8). Each crop sale pays
  `v + ceil(v * b / 100)` at the current headcount (`Game.player_count()`). The host applies it in
  `Crops.sell_bonus` at the sell box (`station.gd`), the dawn moonflower cash-in (`death.gd
  step_cash_in`) and the final-dawn end-of-season sale (`death.gd step_final_sale`). It never touches the
  Prize Pumpkin payout or early payments. Logged: `sell` gains `bonus` (and `coins` includes it); the dawn
  cash-in now emits a `sell` with `dawn: true`; `end_of_season_sale` gains `bonus`.
- **Sim.** `Model.sold(v, hc)` applies the same rounding to day sales, the dawn cash-in (after death
  losses) and the end-season 50% sale, not the payout.
- **Table shipped: 0 at every headcount** (D-106). The sim's median team already meets every s18.3 target
  at 2 to 6 players and the 2p median already pays the first payment (first clear 87.1%, dawn-4 margin
  quartiles 60/70/74 coins). Any 2p bonus that rounds to a coin pushes the 2p final above 70%: 0.01% gives
  70.9, 1% 76.8, 2% 81.5, 5% 90.8 (first 91.3). Full sweep in doc 02 s18.6 "P4-30 sell bonus".
- **Q-161 unattended term: modelled, off by default** (D-107). Policy switch `unattended_term` (false in
  `median.json`) adds `min((300 - 30) // 60, 3)` = 3 tramples on nobody-outside nights. With it on, final
  clear falls to 32.6/26.7/26.2/26.2/26.5 at 2p to 6p and Large needed fails everywhere. Sized under the
  term: 2p alone needs 3 to 5% (5% gives 89.7/67.2) at the median 20% hide rate; hiding every night (the
  D-089 world, about 5 tramples a night) 2p needs 10% for the first payment (85.3) and its final stays 0.2.
  An economy-wide 5/5/4/4/4 passes first and final at all but 5p (72.1). No bonus fixes Large needed under
  the term. That retune is the Director's call (Q-211); the CEO's choice is Q-210.
