# P5-63 walls clipping, floating cans

## Found
- Floating cans (root cause): `game/items/cans.gd` spawned cans at the prop node's `global_position`. Prop boxes
  (`box()` in build_farm.py) put the node origin at the box centre, so the Well (h 1.0) and FuelDrum (h 1.0) gave y 0.5:
  all 2 water cans (well 49,-20 full farm; -25,10 Phase 1) and the fuel can (-10,27) floated 0.5 m. Fix: spawn at y 0.
- Building walls: `building()` N/S walls ended at the W/E wall centreline and W/E walls ran centreline to centreline, so each
  corner had a 0.15 x 0.3 m overlap (coplanar tops, z-fighting in the gray box / Phase 1 scene) and a notch. Barn, Farmhouse, ToolShed (4 corners each).
  Fix: N/S walls run the full outer width, W/E fill between inner faces.
- Pen fence (-30..-18, -38..-28): West/East sections overlapped North/South sections by 0.1 m at all 4 corners. Fix: shortened
  West/East (4.9 m) and South (4.9 m) so they butt.
- Other props: Generator, FuelDrum, Well, ShippingCrate, TownStand, SellBox boxes all sit on y 0 (check below). Pegboard is
  a wall prop at 1.2 m by design. No other floating prop found.
