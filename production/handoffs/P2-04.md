## Done

- **Voice choice** (`game/creature/creature.gd`, `_choose_voice`). Each lure picks a clip, a world
  sound or the stranger by `rand_weighted`: dead teammate 3, alive teammate 1, the target's own voice
  0.1 (doc 03 s12.1). An owner is a candidate only if their setting is Lobby lines and they have a
  clip that fits (`Voice.clips.clip_ids`); Off and unchosen players are never voiced. Name clips
  (`name_<uid>`) log `line_id` `name:<uid>` and never say the hearer's own name. Exact clips only:
  splicing waits for day 4 and the read clock (doc 03 s12.1), not built.
- **Day and night lures.** Night lures play for everyone at a crow perch or creature cover 12 to 40 m
  away (a non-lone target: near an armed trap). Day lures start in the second third of the day, at a
  trap spot, go to the target only, and need every other teammate 15 m from the source (doc 03 s12.2,
  `DAY_RULE_M`). Day gap 90 s (`DAY_LURE_GAP_S`, placeholder until the AI Director budget, DD Phase 3).
- **Where a teammate could not be.** A clip source is at least 25 m from its living owner
  (`COULD_NOT_BE_M`, inference from s12.2 "somewhere the teammate could not be"); no such spot means
  the stranger.
- **Tells.** One tell or none, about 1/3 none (s12.2); `no_crackle` added to `TELLS`. Clips play as an
  `AudioStreamPlayer3D` through `VoiceChain.bus_for(tell)` and `attach_crackle` (Audio P2-08), loaded
  only if `game/audio/voice_chain.gd` exists, else on `VoiceBase` with no tell effect.
- **Stops.** Each peer checks the owner's setting and packets at play time (`lure_skipped`,
  `why` `owner_off` or `missing`) and stops a playing clip on `Voice.clips.clip_freed`
  (`lure_stopped`). Clips never touch the receiver's disk.
- **Logs.** `lure_played` names `lure_id`, `kind` (clip/sound/stranger), `owner`, `line_id`,
  `clip_id`, `sound_id`, `tell`, `heard_by`, `ghost`, `day`, `exact`. `lure_result` unchanged from
  Phase 1. `lure_fooled` (CONTRACTS s10, D-020) logged when a clip lure worked.
- **`check_logs.py`** prints recorded (clip) vs generic lure rates, marked "doc 09 s3, read not gated".
- **QA flag** `--creature-walk`: the creature walker with the real clock, so lures run under
  `multi.py` (see doc 03 s12.4).
