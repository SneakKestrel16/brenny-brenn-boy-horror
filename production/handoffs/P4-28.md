# P4-28: a bigger watering can against the money gap
Owner: Game Designer  Date: 2026-10-09

Status: in review. No data change: `data/labor.json` `can.capacity` stays 2 (doc 02 s3, placeholder).

## Answer

No. A bigger can does not fix the money gap. In the sim it helps only the small teams, and there it
helps too much: 3 or 4 plots per fill pushes the 2p final clear to 88% and the 3p final clear to 73 to
75%, which breaks doc 02 s18.3 (55 to 70%, at most 10 points spread). At 4p to 6p it changes almost
nothing, because there the plot count limits the team, not the labor. The bot seasons lose by 368 to
949 coins at the final payment; a bigger can moves the sim's 3p and 4p median final margin by 4 to 42
coins. Keep the can at 2 and look elsewhere for the gap (see "What would close the gap").

s18.3 target checks (`sim.evaluate`, 17 checks):
- **Can 2: 17 of 17 pass** (today's data).
- **Can 3: 13 of 17.** Fails: final clear 2p 88.1, final clear 3p 73.1, final-clear spread 27.5 points,
  Large pumpkin needed at 2p (Medium drops final clear only 2.6 points, needs 30 or more).
- **Can 4: 13 of 17.** Same four fails: 87.9, 75.0, spread 27.3, Medium drop 0.7.

Why it splits by headcount: planting capacity is `players x plots per player` (sim.py `cap`), and
plots owned stop at 16 to start and 24 at most (2p to 4p, `data/player_scaling.json`). At 2p, 2 x 7 = 14
is below the 16 plots owned, so labor limits the team and a faster can adds plots. At 4p, 4 x 7 = 28
already exceeds the 24-plot ceiling, so a faster can adds no plots. At 2p the extra labor is worth
so much that the Prize Pumpkin size stops mattering, which breaks the doc 01 rule that the Large pumpkin
is needed.

## Bot and human seasons (inference)

Compared with the P4-18 handoff and the P4-21 handoff (worktree `agent-a43e44af42bf6b65a`, in review):
- P4-21 normal seasons: 3p final short 368 and 401, 4p short 484 and 949, 2p short 876 and 932 (and the
  first payment missed). P4-18 batch 2: final 0 paid of 931 to 1,664 owed.
- The bigger can moves the sim median final margin by +34 (can 3) or +42 (can 4) at 3p and +4 at 4p.
  That is under 12% of the smallest 3p gap and under 1% of the 4p gaps. At 2p it moves +106 or +146,
  under 17% of an ~900 gap.

**Inference**, not measured: the bot gap is not a watering gap. The P4-21 handoff names the likely
causes: an idle host (the debt counts `n` players, only `n-1` bots work; Q-162), no plot or upgrade
buys, a dead generator every night (+1 trample), moonflower seeds bought from the payment money, and
Harvest Moon deaths. None of these is labor per plot, so a faster can cannot reach them. The 2p bot
team is one bot (the other "player" is the idle host), so it is closer to a 1-player team than the
sim's 2p, and still a can would not cover ~900 coins. What would settle it: the human seasons in
`tools/qa/playtest/checklist_p4.md` with `sim.py compare`, or a bot season with `can.capacity` 3 set
locally (not committed) to see whether sales rise by more than the sim's +34 to +42 at 3p.

## What would close the gap (for the Director)

Not part of this task; no data changed. In order of likely size, from the P4-21 numbers:
1. Settle Q-162 (how bot seasons count the idle host). A 3p bot season is really 2 workers against a 3p
   debt.
2. Bots buy plot pairs from day 4 like the sim policy (`buy_plots_from_day` 4,
   `tools/sim/policies/median.json`).
3. Bots refuel the generator (QA finding 3 in P4-21) and keep the moonflower reserve (QA finding 1).
4. Only after the human seasons: if humans also miss by hundreds, retune the economy as a whole with the
   sim (debt, crop prices), not one labor knob.

## How to reproduce

The sim has no flag for can capacity, so this sets it in memory and calls the sim's own functions
(no copy of its logic). Run from the repo root:

```
uv run --no-project python -I -c "import sys; sys.path.insert(0,'tools/sim'); import sim
L=sim.load_json(sim.HERE/'layout.json'); P=sim.load_json(sim.HERE/'policies'/'median.json'); C=[2,3,4,5,6]
for cap in (2,3,4):
    d=sim.load_data(); d['labor']['can']['capacity']=cap
    s={'scenarios':{x['name']:sim.run_scenario(d,L,P,x,C,10000,1)[0] for x in sim.resolve_scenarios(None)}}
    print(cap,{p:s['scenarios']['baseline'][p]['final_clear_pct'] for p in C},[(n,v) for n,v,ok in sim.evaluate(s,C) if not ok])"
```

The default run (`uv run --no-project python -I tools/sim/sim.py`) still prints 17 PASS with today's
data.
