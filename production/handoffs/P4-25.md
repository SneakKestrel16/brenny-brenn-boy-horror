# P4-25 Creature back to the corn at dawn, barn pin, lit-barn kill, Harvest Moon with nobody pushing (AI Programmer)

## Root causes
- **Stuck in the barn (OPEN_ISSUES "CEO's 2-instance session" item 7).** The creature had no
  pathfinding: `_move` steered straight at `_goal` with `move_and_slide`. On night 1 the AI Director
  nudged it to `yard`; the line from (-14, 8) to `cover_01` (13, -6) runs in through the barn door, and
  it pinned on the inside of the east wall at (7.4, -4.5) (reproduced exactly, (7.449, -4.47)). At dawn
  `_night_t` went to -1 with the state still `retreat`, and the day-cover goal (30, 57), straight through
  the wall, kept it pinned all day.
- **Kill in the lit barn (CEO, night 2, `instance_1.log` lines 1292-1335).** Night 2 ran the scripted
  sequence; its stalk-to-chase (`_run_script`) chases the true position and had **no lit-building
  check**. The generator was powered (the tank refills at `day_changed`). The whistle at (-0.4, -3.7)
  only coincided; it is not the cause. The hunting chase also checked the catch before
  `_in_lit_doorway`, and only the 6 m doorway counted, not the building interior.
- **Harvest Moon, nobody pushing (CEO).** In act 2 the creature picked only pushers; with nobody
  pushing it fell back to the loudest sound, but the knock lunge needs `cart.pushers.has(p)`, so it held
  a stalk at the 10 m standoff for ever. The AI Director (`allow`) also refused every act 2 chase. A
  second bug made it worse: after a knock-off, `_set_state(&"retreat")` cleared `_goal` **before** the
  30 m back-off was computed from it, so the back-off was `INF - pos` (Godot "cannot be normalized"
  warning) and it stood still for the whole 30 s retreat.

## Done
- `_move` steers around buildings: `_way_to` goes through the door (a step outside, then a step
  inside) when the goal or the creature is in a building, else to the clear corner of the nearest
  building on the line. `_shut_out` turns a goal inside a lit building into a point 6 m outside its door
  (`LIT_DOOR_M`), so it never walks into the light. Doors come from the same scan as `_rects`.
- Dawn: `_to_corn()` puts it at the AI Director's day cover if it is not in a `corn_ring*` region, sets
  `lurk`, clears memory, and logs `creature_dawn_reset {from, to, region}`.
- No kill in a lit building: `_sheltered(pos)` (generator powered and inside a building or its lit
  doorway) ends both the scripted and the hunting chase as `lit_building` before any catch.
- Harvest Moon acts 2 and 3 with nobody pushing: it goes for the living player it senses nearest the
  cart (sensed positions only, never inside a lit building) and chases them as at night, so a catch
  kills; knock cooldown and stall bites unchanged. `ai_director.allow(&"chase")` allows that chase in
  act 2 only while the cart has no pushers. The knock-off back-off now runs (goal saved before
  `_set_state`).
- Clients snap the creature body if it is more than 10 m (`SNAP_M`) from the host's position (the dawn
  reset is a teleport).
- Doc 03 section 14: new "Nobody pushing" bullet (Q-185 asks the Game Designer to review it).
