# P5-65 Creature stood still during the day (AI Programmer)

## Root cause

`game/creature/creature.gd` `_move()` set `_goal` to the day cover on **every** frame whenever no night
was running (`_night_t < 0.0`). That covered every day state and the whole day. Nothing by day ever picked
a lurk wander point, so the creature walked to `cover_15` (30, 57), stood there all day and could not leave.
The line goes back to the "fake it first" day (Phase 1 "Day: it waits in far cover"), so P5-56 and P5-57 did
not cause it. The bug is on every path, not only single player.

CEO log `logs/ceo_single.log` (season 20261010_155809_2191, day 1), main repo:

```
112:[log] creature_state {"from":"lurk","position":[30.0,0.0,57.0],"reason":"dev","target":1,"to":"lurk"}
162:[log] creature_state {"from":"lurk","position":[30.0,0.0,57.0],"reason":"dev","target":1,"to":"chase"}
219:[log] creature_state {"from":"chase","position":[30.0,0.0,57.0],"reason":"dev","target":1,"to":"retreat"}
307:[log] creature_state {"from":"retreat","position":[30.0,0.0,57.0],"reason":"dev","target":1,"to":"chase"}
345:[log] creature_state {"from":"chase","position":[30.0,0.0,57.0],"reason":"night","target":null,"to":"lurk"}
```

The position stays the same across 230 log lines of day 1, even with dev-forced chases. At night it moves (line 432 on).

## What doc 03 says

Doc 03 s4 `lurk` says it "wanders its region at walk speed". Doc 01 "Behavior states" says Lurk "moves
through corn". Doc 03 s4.2 says "By day only lurk, lure, stalk and the jumpscare apply". Doc 03 s11.3 day
arc says third 1 is calm with "no presence" and keeps out of 25 m of the worked field. So by day it should
move, through the corn, and not be absent.

## Fix

In `_move()`, a day `lurk` (and dusk/dawn, where `third()` is 0) now calls `_wander()`, from the day's
second third. That reuses the night's P5-56 corn routes, linger and anti-repeat rules. Third 1 keeps the
old day-cover goal, so the calm window and the AI Director's field keep-out (`day_cover`) still hold.
**Inference** (marked in code): third 1 parks rather than wanders. Doc 03 s11.3 "no presence" supports
it, but a playtest settles it. Other day states (trap race excepted, which has its own branch) still go to
the day cover, as before.
