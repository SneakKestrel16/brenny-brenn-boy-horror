## What was done

### 1. Noise and night targeting

**Cause (from `logs/playtest_20261009/` and seeded headless nights).** The creature heard the players, but
the AI Director refused the hunt. A group that talks and runs keeps the tension meter high, so the night sat
in fade (waiting for the meter to fall to `fade_to` 30) and then in relax (waiting for an empty meter). Stalk
is only allowed in build-up and peak, and chase only in peak (doc 03 s11.1), so most of the night no hunt
could start. In build-up, a creature that heard a noise but was not allowed to stalk stayed in Lurk.

**Fix.**
- `data/ai_director.json` `phases`: new caps `fade_max_s` 20 and `relax_max_s` 40 (placeholders, cite
  CEO STOP 6). `director_logic.gd` `next_phase` leaves fade and relax at the cap.
- `creature.gd`: in Lurk during build-up, when a stalk is not allowed, the creature walks to the position it
  heard. It still hunts only by what it senses (doc 03 s4.2).

**Measure** (`tests/creature/test_p5_33_night.gd`, one night, 3 bots, seeds 1 to 3):

| | fade + relax share of the night | unscripted hunts |
|---|---|---|
| before (`night_before_s{1,2,3}.log`) | 66 %, 79 %, 63 % | 1 to 2 |
| after (`night_after_s{1,2,3}.log`) | 55 %, 49 %, 49 % | 5, 5, 4 |

Final seed 1 (`night_final_s1.log`): build-up 76 s, peak 60 s, fade 60 s, relax 103 s, 5 hunts, PASS.

### 2. Bear trap

**Cause (playtest `host.jsonl`, all 4 trap deaths).**
- All 4 races ended in death at about 7.7 s, which is 27 m at 3.5 m/s. The victim had almost no time.
- 3 of the 4 traps were sprung at night. Doc 01 says the race starts "by day", but `starts_race_by_day`
  was never read, so every spring started a clock.
- At t=2207 a teammate finished a pry and the code ignored it, because only the victim's own pry counted.
- The clock killed while the creature was 66 to 72 m away, or still in Lurk. The death did not match
  anything the players could see, so it felt random.
- The victim had to aim at the trap's small pick box to pry it.
- By day the creature's body stayed in cover and never came.

**Fix.**
- Race start distances 32 m and deep 25 m (were 25 and 18): solo spare 5.1 s, deep 3.1 s at 3.5 m/s with a
  4 s pry (doc 03 s7.2 derivation and table).
- Only a day spring starts a race. A night pin has no clock. It kills only when the creature really catches
  the victim (`caught`, cause `night_trap`).
- Any living player's pry frees the victim. A teammate's pry is logged as helped.
- When the race starts, the creature body is placed `start_m` away and runs at the victim in Chase, so the
  threat is visible and audible. `creature.gd` `race_approach` and an adaptive speed in `_move`: it speeds
  up to chase speed when the way round buildings is longer, so it reaches the victim on the deadline.
- A pinned player pries their own trap with E, without aiming (`hold_controller.gd`).
- HUD (`hud.gd` `_trap_text`): the victim sees a countdown by day and "It can hear you" at night;
  teammates see an alert with the distance.

**Measure** (`tests/creature/test_p5_33_trap.gd`, `trap_after.log`, PASS):
- A: start 33.4 m, deadline 9.54 s; the creature closed 7.1 m in 2 s; teammate pry 4.02 s; survived with
  3.51 s spare.
- B: no help, died at 9.53 s with the creature 1.0 m away.
- C: night pin, no clock; the catch kills.
- Before: 7.7 s deaths with the creature 66 to 72 m away (playtest log).

2 instances: `logs/p5_33/multi/` (night pin, `race: false`, MULTI PASS), `multi_day/` and `multi_day2/`
(day race, MULTI PASS).

### 3. Crows

- `scares.gd` `_crows`: silent flyovers (every 40 to 90 s) and field landings (every 60 to 120 s, on
  `plot_spots`) by day and dusk, on their own seeded stream (`seed + 6`), so scare rolls do not change. A
  landing flock flies off when a living player comes within 8 m. Numbers are in `ai_director.json` `crows`
  (placeholders). The flocks are public `apply_scare` sends (`crow_flyover`, `crow_land`), so every peer
  draws the same flock. They call no sound and are not scares (no budget). They log `crows`.
- P5-27 finding fixed: a fake-out burst hides the still crow on its perch for `perch_empty_s` 60 s, so the
  burst is the crow that sat there.

**Measure** (`tests/creature/test_p5_33_crows.gd`, seed 1, `crows_after.log`, PASS): day 2 and its dusk,
600 s, gave 8 flyovers and 5 landings (1.30 per minute), most crow models at once 10. Forced fake-out at
perch `crow_01`: still crow hidden; back after 62 s. Before: no flyovers or landings existed; crows were the
9 still perched ones plus a fake-out about once per 240 s.
