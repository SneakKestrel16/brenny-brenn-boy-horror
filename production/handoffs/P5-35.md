# P5-35 Player models break when tainted

## Cause
P5-23 stained the world body's own sleeves (`farmer_body.gd` `stain`). `TaintLook.show_on` still also hung the old
P3-08 pair of `tool_hands` under every non-local player's camera, scaled 1.4x and following the camera pitch. Other
players' bodies therefore grew a second, floating pair of black arms/hands that detached from the body whenever the
owner looked up or down. `production/handoffs/P5-35/before.png`: stray blue hand blobs beside the red and blue bodies;
the yellow body (camera pitched down 40 degrees) shows them away from the body. Shown whenever `tainted` is true, so
every Taint stage. Hat and overalls colour on the body are fine (checked with a top hat plus overalls colour; the
sleeve override leaves other surfaces alone).

## Fix
`game/render/taint_look.gd`: `show_on` returns early for non-local players after `body.stain(on)`. The camera arms
exist only for the local player (`_arms()` lost its `local` argument and the non-local pose). Local screen smudge and
arms unchanged.

## Verification
Windowed render (not headless) of three bodies through `TaintLook.show_on` with stub players (cameras at head height,
one yawed, one pitched). Before: `production/handoffs/P5-35/before.png`. After: `production/handoffs/P5-35/after.png`
(black sleeves on the bodies only). Not run: a full 2-instance host/client session; the helper runs the same code on every peer.

## Playtest logs
`logs/playtest_20261009/2/godot.log`: only error is 48x `Lambda capture at index 0 was freed` (around
clip_manifest/clip_received, voice clips). Not Taint related, not fixed here.

  `tint()` reads fine but is untested windowed.
