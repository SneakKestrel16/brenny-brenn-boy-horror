## Design numbers

- 3 seasons per campaign; lost season ends it. Carry: upgrades, plots (clamped to the headcount ceiling), savings
  = floor(25% of spare after final payment and cosmetics), cap 60.
- Debt (4p): season 1 1,300, season 2 1,595, season 3 1,600, with per-headcount bases (2p 1,335/1,355, 3p
  1,465/1,510, 5p 1,570/1,575, 6p 1,560/1,565). First payment 255 at 4p.
- Ten creature traits, one per season from season 2, picked by host seed + season, logged `trait_gained`.
- Cosmetics: hats 15 to 70, overalls 15 to 60, sold once the debt is paid, no headcount scaling, kept per roster id.
- Imposter: 50%, one, min 4 players, never kills, keeps `still_honest` signals. Ten quirks with numbers in doc 02 22.2.
