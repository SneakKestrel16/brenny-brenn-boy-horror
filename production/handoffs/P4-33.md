# P4-33 handoff: flag limit, removal and minimap icons

Gameplay Programmer, 2026-10-09. Source: CEO 2026-10-09 ("players should have a limit on the amount of
flags they can place and add a method to remove one they already placed, flags should up on the minimap
as well as small icons"); scope add D-141 ("dont show players on the minimap"). Decision D-120.

## What was done

- **Owner per flag.** `TrapSweep.flags` is now one `{pos: Vector3, by: peer}` per flag (was a bare
  `Vector3`), sent whole by `apply_flags` as before (`net.gd` unchanged; its Array argument carries the
  dictionaries). New host helpers: `limit()`, `count_of(peer)`, `own_flag_at(pos, peer)`,
  `remove_flag(i, peer)`.
- **Limit (host).** `FlagSpot.can_start(place_flag)` refuses `flag_limit` when the player already has
  `labor.json` `place_flag.max_per_player` flags out (3, `placeholder`; doc 01 now says "only a few").
  The schema gains the optional integer `max_per_player` on a hold record.
- **Removal (host-validated).** Every peer gives each drawn flag a `FlagSpot` with a layer-4 pick body.
  The owner aiming at it sees "Hold E: Pull up your flag" (`verbs_for` offers `remove_flag` only when
  `by == Game.local_peer()`); the request goes through `request_hold` like any hold. The host
  re-checks ownership (`not_your_flag`) and frees the slot. `remove_flag` is 0.5 s in `INSTANT_S`
  (placeholder, like `take_shovel`). A flag cleared by a trap disarm or fill also frees its slot.
- **Log.** `flag_placed` and the new `flag_removed` carry `player`, `position`, `trap_id` (null), `flags`
  (all out), `mine` (the player's count). Refusals log as `hold_refused`.
- **Minimap.** `minimap.gd` `_draw_dyn` draws each flag as a small red pennant on a pale pole (about
  7 px) at its current position, every frame, for every peer. A future creature move shows at once.
- **D-141.** Teammate dots are gone from the minimap. It shows only the layout, the cart, the local
  arrow and flags. No test checked the dots (`grep minimap tests tools`: none).
- **HUD** text for `remove_flag`, `flag_limit`, `not_your_flag`.
- **Docs.** Doc 01 "Night Traps > Flags" (CEO change, D-120). Doc 05: flags paragraph, the "No HUD
  markers" minimap text (flags in, teammates out), the `flag_placed`/`flag_removed` event row.
