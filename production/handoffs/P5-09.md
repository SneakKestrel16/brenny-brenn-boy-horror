# P5-09 Quirks (Gameplay Programmer, with the AI Programmer parts)

Opt-in group option (doc 01 "Quirks", D-052, D-158). Numbers are all in `data/quirks.json`; no number is in code.
D-161 (CEO answer to Q-252 item 4): quirks are revealed at season end, built here.

## What changed

- `game/player/quirks.gd` (class `Quirks`): ids, effects, the pure seeded draw, host `sync`, `reroll(season)`,
  `set_on`, `effect(peer, key)`, `carry_cap(st)`, client `mine` / `local(key)`, pure `reveal`.
- Assignment: host draws at match start (and for a rejoiner or latecomer) without replacement in the team, seeded by
  `Game.seed_value` + `Quirks.season_n` + the sorted roster uids. Kept in `Game.quirks` (uid -> id), so a rejoiner
  keeps theirs and the save carries it (`quirks` key plus the `quirks_on` game flag). Ghosts have none (`of`); `held`
  still reads it (Narcolepsy at dawn). Log `quirk_assigned` {uid, quirk, season, seed}.
- Sync: no new RPC (net.gd is not mine). `Roles.sync` calls `Quirks.sync`, which sends each peer only its own quirk
  and the option as a partial `apply_roles` table; `Roles.apply` hands it to `Quirks.apply`. A client never learns
  another player's quirk until the season-end card.
- Lobby: "Quirks" checkbox (host only, off by default, D-158). `--quirks` turns it on for QA.
- Season-end reveal: `SeasonAwards._host_end` adds `quirks` lines ("Name: Disorder") to the payload it already sends;
  `show_card` fades them in under the awards. Only with the option on.
- HUD: own quirk only, one status line ("Quirk: X (only you can see this)", `data` `self_only_hud`).
- Effects (each cites its data record):
  - Anxiety disorder: Shaken x2 (`trap_race.shake`, host), sprint refill x2 faster (player.gd, client).
  - Nyctophobia: `store.lantern_mult` x0.5 (nothing draws the player lantern yet, so no visible change; creature
    sight of the lantern is untouched).
  - ADHD: `NoiseBus.emit_voice` radius x1.5 for that speaker (no extra lure weight).
  - Paranoia: `game/player/quirk_watch.gd`, local only: 4 phantom steps 3 m behind, every 150 to 300 s, at most 3 a
    day, night too, through Soundscape only. Never NoiseBus, never sent, no light or radio, no tension.
  - Schizophrenia: `scares.gd _day_for` opens `hallucination` on day 1 for that player; weight and the big-scare
    budget unchanged.
  - Dyspraxia: jumpscare also drops shovel/trap and empties the bag (`scares.gd fire`).
  - Hoarding disorder: bag of 5 (`plot.gd` `bag_full`, host; HUD count), walk and crouch x0.9 (client; sprint unchanged).
  - Narcolepsy: `death.gd dawn` respawns that body 6 s after the others (`respawn` already checks `_dead`).
  - Grandiose delusions: `trap_race._on_sprung` waits 0.6 s; pinned only if still within 1 m of the trap, else
    `_loosen(.., "dodged")` and log `quirk_dodge`. The sprung broadcast and the creature's click happen at the step.
  - OCD: host check in `quirk_watch.gd`: 2 s in a scarecrow's 120 degree front arc within 6 m gives Shaken (never
    Taint), 120 s cooldown per scarecrow per player. Log `quirk_ocd_gaze`.
- Photosensitivity: nothing here touches a light or flickers anything.
- Main builds one `QuirkWatch` node after TrapRace. `Data.TABLES` gained `quirks`.
