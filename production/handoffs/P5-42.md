# P5-42 Big heads texture (Gameplay)

## Cause
P5-35 did not fix it. `big_heads` (`DevToys`) called `Player.set_head_scale`, which scaled a separate white `_head` sphere mesh, not the farmer model. With the rigged farmer (P5-13) the sphere floated over the real head, hid the hat and showed no texture; the real head stayed small below it. Screenshots: `production/handoffs/P5-42/before_host.png`, `before_client.png`.

## Fix
`game/player/head_scaler.gd` (new): a `SkeletonModifier3D` that sets the `head` bone pose scale after the animation each frame. `FarmerBody.set_head_scale(s)` adds it on first use. The skinned head texture and the child `hat` bone (cosmetic hats) now scale together. `Player.set_head_scale` calls it; the old sphere, its height/visibility handling are removed. `dev_toys.gd` is unchanged (P5-41 owns the nuke there). Doc 05 updated ("Big heads (P5-42)").

## Proof
Windowed 2 instances (host + client, `--audio-driver Dummy`, `-- --free-mouse` last), hats (top_hat, bucket_hat), plaid and striped overalls, Taint stain, `toy big_heads`: `after_host.png`, `after_client.png` show the textured 3x head with its hat, no z-fighting, no detached face, on both peers.
Tests: `test_dev_toys_sync.gd` PASS (2 instances). Headless import: no SCRIPT ERROR; the only ERRORs are "Cannot go into subdir" from the copied `.godot` cache of this worktree (not from the change).
