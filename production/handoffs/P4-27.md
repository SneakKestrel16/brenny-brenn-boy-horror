## What was done

- **Cover, tree lines, landmarks** (doc 04 s14, new): 39 trees in 8 groups (orchard, two windbreak rows
  north of field A, north-east clump, east tree line, west clump, south grove), split-rail fences round
  both fields, the moonflower bed and the gate, 10 signposts, and six landmarks (silo north, water tower
  south, windpump at the well, arch over the farm gate, hay stack, wood pile). All built by
  `game/world/build_farm.py` into `farm.tscn` only; `farm_phase1.tscn` is byte-identical.
- **Only tree canopies collide**, on layer 5 (corn sight-blocker), from 1.2 m up. Trunks, fences, paths,
  signs and landmarks are visual only. Reason: players and the creature both use collision mask 1, and the
  creature has no navmesh or unstuck logic, so a solid obstacle could pin it (D-100 proposed, Q-196).
  Canopies block creature sight (eye 1.65 m); the 1 m queries (hearing ray, ghost `_in_corn`, sprint-in-corn
  step) pass under them.
- **Placement rules enforced in `check_farm.gd` `_cover()`**: each canopy edge keeps the work spot's doc 04
  s8.4 corn distance (pumpkin 20 m), 2 m from every marker and plot, 3 m from every cart route leg, 1 m from
  every s8.7 walk line, and stays out of the spatial audio band. All 39 pass; `check_farm: PASS`.
- **Dirt path on the cart route** (doc 04 s6.1): a 3 m flat visual strip per leg drawn from the same
  `ROUTE` list as `CartRoute`, R0 to R8, then on to the town stand. If the route moves, the path follows.
  Side paths (1.6 m) link barn, well, farmhouse, shed, pumpkin, pen, crate and moonflowers.
- **Cart hover, shared cause, fixed**: `game/items/cart.gd` raised the `prop_cart.glb` model 0.86 m
  (`m.position.y = 0.86`), but the glb origin is at the wheel base (AABB y 0.025..1.75, bed floor vertices
  y 0.87 to 0.9). The body sits on `CartRoute` at y 0 both parked (`_place` at offset 0) and pushed
  (`_place` every frame), so the same offset floated it everywhere. Removed the line; `BED_Y` 0.9 is
  unchanged and matches the bed floor. Cross-path edit in the Gameplay Programmer's file, filed as Q-195.
- **Doc 04**: s14 added, s6.1 path bullet, s2 legend row, s11 two gotchas, Contents lists s13 and s14.
  `04_farm_layout.svg`: tree canopies drawn at collider radius; legend moved 100 px east (it covered the
  east tree line) with a tree row.
