# P5-56 handoff: smarter creature AI (AI Programmer)

CEO asked to "improve the AI of the creature as much as possible" (2026-10-10). The coordinator added
Q-354: on bot nights the creature stood in the open clearing 806 of 900 night seconds. This pass fixes
how lurk moves, how a lost chase searches, where night traps go, and two stalls found while measuring.
Hunting still uses only sensed positions (doc 01 "The AI Director": "Hunting (movement, pursuit, trap
placement) uses only the creature's senses and Corruption"). Day/night rules, state tells, death rules,
door bangs and the creature's door collision exception are unchanged.

## For CEO review

Every behaviour change is one number in `data/creature.json`, `source: "placeholder"`. Set it to 0 to
revert that change alone; no code edit is needed.

| Change | Flag (creature.json) | Value | Revert |
|---|---|---|---|

Unflagged fixes (they remove stalls, not rules):
- **Stuck walk.** A lurk walk that moves under a fifth of its speed for 2 s drops its goal and route
  (`creature_stuck`). The pen fence held it 218 s in a probe run; `_way_to` knows buildings, not fences.
- **Harvest Moon region check.** Acts 2 and 3 tested the current hop against the AI Director's region,
  so every corn route was dropped at once and it stood at cover_01 (183 idle samples on seed 3). It now
  tests the route's end, within 25 m of the region. Acts 2 and 3 also walk straight as before
  (`_wander(true)`): the finale should press the cart, not hide.

Not done, on purpose:
- **Per-body behaviour.** Doc 01 "Bodies": "They all hunt identically". Q-355 (FOR CEO) asks about doc 01
  open issue 4.
- **Chase flanking or leading the target.** It would cut the escape that doc 03 s5 promises (break line of
  sight and go quiet); a fairness change for the CEO to ask for, not to slip in.
- **Lure timing.** `_play_lure` already targets lone players and the AI Director budgets it; no weakness
  found in the runs.
- **Light rules.** Unchanged: P5-39 added the bang before a dark building; no new fault found.

Trade-off for the CEO (Q-356): a creature in corn hears less (doc 03 s3 `corn_damp_mult` cuts a radius
by 30% through corn) and the corn ring is far from where players work, so it stalks less. See the numbers.
