# P5-74 Pacing evaluation (Game Designer)

Report only. No `data/` value changed. All proposals need a CEO decision.

## Method

- 4 bot seasons, seeds 1-4, 3 players (idle host + `--bots=2`), `--time-scale 8`, headless. Command:
  `godot --headless --audio-driver Dummy --path . --time-scale 8 -- --host --bots=2 --lobby-start=1 --no-intro --port=<49xxx> --free-mouse --seed=<n>`.
- Logs: `peer_1.jsonl` per run (kept in `%TEMP%\p574\b2_s{1..4}`). Scratch analysers: `logs/p574_an*.py`, `logs/p574_sim.py` (gitignored).
- Sim what-ifs: `tools/sim/sim.py` model, 3p, 3000 runs, seed 1, median policy (`tools/sim/policies/median.json`).
- Caveats: log `t` is wall-clock at time-scale 8 (x8 for game seconds); `chase_s` is game seconds. Only 4 runs, one headcount. Runs are not deterministic at time-scale 8. Tension-phase fractions come from 10 s samples; absolute seconds did not reconcile with game time, so use fractions only.

## Result

All 4 seasons ended day 7 on `final_payment` loss. First payment (217 at 3p, doc 01 Debt table) was made at dawn 3 in all 4. `season_lost.short`: seed 1 470, seed 2 624, seed 3 497, seed 4 601.

Root answer: two independent causes each sink the season. Harvest Moon gate-run wipes (3 of 4 seeds) remove the cart income. Even without a wipe (seed 2) the bot economy is 624 coins short of the final. The creature on nights 1-6 is not the problem.

## (a) Creature difficulty

Source: `chase_ended`, `death`, `creature_state`, `cart_finished` events.

- Nights 1-6, 24 nights across 4 seeds: 1 death total (seed 4, night 1). Chases 0-1 per night, about 3-10 s each. Stalks 0-4 per night.
- Day 7 Harvest Moon: 3 deaths in each of seeds 1, 3, 4. Seed 2 got the cart out with 0 deaths.
- Time units: log `t` is wall-clock at `--time-scale 8`; multiply `t` differences by 8 for game seconds. `chase_ended.chase_s` is already game seconds (seed 1: `t` 779.5 to 780.3 = 0.8 s wall = 6.4 game s = `chase_s` 6.4). Earlier drafts quoted wall seconds (0.1-3.5 s); that was wrong.
- Mechanism (read from code, not inferred): act 3 (`GATE_RUN`, last `gate_run_m` 30 m) has no knock-offs or stalls. `cart.knock_ready()` needs `act == PUSH`, so `knock_stall_s` and `knock_cooldown_s` never apply in act 3. `creature.gd` 475-477 starts a normal `chase` with reason `gate_run` that kills on catch (doc 03 s14). `ai_director.gd _profile_allows` gives act 3 an unlimited chase allowance (no per-player count). `ordinary_traps` is false in acts 2-3. What can stop it: a flare (`retreat_after_flare_s` 30, needs the 50-coin gun and shells), a pusher it loses (`chase_lose_quiet_s`), or never being heard/seen (seed 2 had no gate chase at all and cleared at x 104.9, 23 game s after act 3 began).
- Kill timing from act 3 start, game seconds (seeds 1, 3, 4; chase start distance `start_m` in brackets): seed 1 first death 6.4 s (30.2 m), second 16.8 s, at x 81.2 then x 70.1; seed 3 first death 6.4 s (31.4 m), second 15.2 s, at x 81.4 then x 69.2; seed 4 first death 5.6 s (14.9 m start, second chase 1.5 m), second 8 s, at x 83.1 and 85.4. A 30 m chase at 5.5 m/s is about 5.5 s, matching the 6.4 s first kill.
- Why the cart stops: the second pusher dies at x 69-70 (seeds 1, 3), so no pushers are left and the cart stops at x 83-87 where it coasted. It does not stall.
- Run length: 30 m at cart speed 2.0 m/s (3p) is 15 s; at 2.4 m/s (4p) it is 12.5 s; at 1.6 m/s (2p) 18.8 s. Chase `creature.json chase_speed_mps` 5.5 catches a 30 m start in about 6-9 s even while the target moves away, so the run is not survivable unflared with faster carts alone: at 2.4 m/s a 30 m start is caught in about 9.7 s against a 12.5 s run (QA figure, consistent with the logs).
- Idle host stands at barn spawn (-3,-8). A `no_pushers` chase from 12-14 m kills it after the pushers are down, which ends the run (`cart_finished` reason `all_dead`). Test artefact: a human host would not idle.
- Medical bill (102) is charged in wipes and lands on the final.

Finding: creature difficulty is concentrated in the Harvest Moon gate run. Nights 1-6 are tame against bots.

## (b) Economy pressure

Source: `money_changed`, `store_buy`, `harvest`, `trample`, `can_stolen`, `trap_stolen`, `pumpkin_*` events; doc 01 Debt table (3p: 888 final, 217 first).

- Seed 1 money by reason: seeds -929, sell +660, dawn cash-in +780, payment -217, store -30, end sale +80, medical -102. Sum of all `money_changed` deltas is 302 (seeds 1-4: 302, 156, 275, 193; sums excluding the payment: 519, 373, 492, 410). Coins at dawn: 56, 115, 82, 288, 489 (d6).
- Bots spend about 930 on seeds per season (about 100 turnip, 21 moonflower). Day 7 alone is 276 on seeds that cannot ripen. Bot waste.
- Bots keep the first-payment reserve, so day 3 buys 1 turnip and day 4 harvests 1.
- Bots never buy plots (16 all season). Sim median owns 22 (`buy_plots` true from day 4).
- Harvest about 11 turnips per day from 16 plots.
- Theft/sabotage: trample about 2 plots/night (20 farm damage), `can_stolen` 1/night, `trap_stolen` 0-2/night, animals-out costs 18-36 in some seeds. Small next to the shortfall.
- Prize pumpkin: bots never guard (`guarded_nights` 0), so gnaw (`pumpkin.json` `gnaw_from_night` 3, `guard_radius_m` 20) drops size. Judgement: sad in seed 2 (payout 16), medium bitten to sad in seed 1, Large in seed 4 (never delivered). Large pays about 120 at 3p.
- Seed 2: cart out, 0 deaths, still 264 coins vs 888 due.

Sim (3p, 3000 runs; baseline first clear 85.0%, final 58.1%, on target):

| What-if | Final clear | Median margin |
|---|---|---|
| baseline | 58.1% | - |
| pumpkin sad | 1.9% | -95 |
| pumpkin medium | 14.1% | -51 |
| no deaths (0 deaths) | 100% | +248 |
| no buy_plots | 23.3% | -27 |

Sim median bank after payment by dawn: d2 0, d3 76, d4 40, d5 183, d6 194, d7 245, d8 897.

Finding: the economy is tuned so the final needs a Large pumpkin and plot buying (doc 01 Prize Pumpkin "a median team needs a Large pumpkin"). Bots do neither, so they fail by design, not by a broken economy. The sim and the bots disagree because the sim assumes a competent median team. Sad pumpkin alone takes the sim from 58% to 2%, so the gnaw is the highest-leverage economy knob.

## (c) Encounter frequency

Source: `tension`, `inside_at_night`, `creature_state`, scare/lure events.

- Bots are in the barn almost all night (`inside_at_night` about 300 of 300 s). Few encounters is a bot artefact.
- Night tension (10 s samples) fractions: about 89% build_up, 3% peak, 3% fade, 5% relax. Meter rules: `ai_director.json meter` `outside_per_s` 0.4, `peak_at` 70, `peak_max_s` 20, `fade_max_s` 20, `relax_min_s`/`relax_max_s` 40.
- Scares and lures low; 0-4 stalks per night.

Inference: bots keep the meter low-outside, so the Director sits in build-up. A human farming outdoors would see more stalks and chases. Settle with a human or outdoor-bot run and compare chases/night.

## Bot behaviour vs result

- Understate human play: no pumpkin guard, no plot buying, over-buying seeds on day 7, no flee/flare/dodge in the gate run, idle host counted in wipe.
- Overstate safety on nights 1-6 (indoors).
- So: bot economy failure is mostly bot behaviour; the gate-run wipe is partly bot behaviour and partly real (see proposals).

## Ranked proposals (all inference; settle with sim re-run plus a human playtest)

Check each: `uv run --no-project python -I tools/sim/sim.py --players 2,3,4 --runs 3000 --seed 1` (targets: first ~85%, final 55-70%, spread <= 10 points). The sim does not model the gate run beyond its `deaths` policy, so proposals 1-3 are settled by a human or flare-using bot playtest, then by setting the sim `deaths` policy to match.

Not proposed: `knock_stall_s` and `knock_cooldown_s` (act 2 only, `knock_ready()` needs `PUSH`; raising them makes act 2 harder and does nothing in act 3) and cart speed alone (2.4 m/s still loses a 30 m chase: about 9.7 s to catch vs a 12.5 s run).

1. Gate-run chase speed. New key in `ai_director.json profile_harvest_moon`, e.g. `gate_run_chase_speed_mps`, 5.5 (current `creature.json chase_speed_mps`) to 4.0. The AI Programmer wires it into the `gate_run` chase only; nights 1-6 keep 5.5. Expected effect (inference from distance / (chase speed minus cart speed)): a 30 m start is caught after about 15 s at 3p (2.0 m/s) against the 15 s run, and about 19 s at 4p (2.4 m/s) against 12.5 s. Fewer wipes without removing the threat. Settle with a playtest; try 4.0 to 4.5. Placeholder, not a doc 01 number.
2. Pause after a gate-run kill. New key, e.g. `gate_run_post_kill_pause_s`, from 0 (act 3 chase allowance is unlimited, `ai_director.gd _profile_allows`) to 6. Expected effect: the logs show the second death 8-10 game s after the first (seeds 1, 3: 16.8 s and 15.2 s vs 6.4 s for the first); with a pause the last pushers still reach the gate. Alternative: raise the chase start distance in act 3 (creature lurk standoff, AI Programmer). Placeholder key.
3. Flare economy for the gate run. `store.json flare_shell` price 15 to 10 (placeholder), or one free shell for the cart team at act 3 (gameplay change, ask the Gameplay Programmer). A flare gives a 30 s retreat (`creature.json retreat_after_flare_s`, doc 01 Store), twice the 15 s run. Expected effect: one flare at act 3 start clears the run, so a prepared team survives. Bots never fire flares, so the bot result will not move until a bot fires one in act 3.
4. FOR CEO (changes doc 01 numbers, Prize Pumpkin): `pumpkin.json rules gnaw_from_night` 3 to 4, or `max_escort_bites` 2 to 1, or `guard_radius_m` 20 wider. Expected effect: a Large pumpkin is reachable without nightly outdoor guarding; the sim shows the gnaw is the highest-leverage economy knob (sad pumpkin: final clear 1.9%; medium 14.1%). Not `guard_s`: it only sets Giant guarding in `prize_pumpkin.gd`, and in the sim only when `charge_gnaw_guard` is on, which `median.json` has false (doc 02 s18.6). Run the sim: the final clear may go above 70%.
5. FOR CEO (doc 01 number): `season.json start_coins` 60 to 85, or turnip seed price 4 to 3 in `crops.json`. Expected effect: the day 3-4 reserve is less crippling (bots harvest 1 turnip on day 4). Sim must confirm.

If 1-3 push the sim final clear above 70%, move item 1 back toward 4.5 (one knob).

Non-data (route to Gameplay/AI Programmer, not applied): bots guard the pumpkin, buy plots, stop buying seeds on day 7, use flares in the gate run, and exclude an idle host from the wipe test. Re-run P5-72 A/B after these; the bot result is only a pacing signal once the bots play the economy.
